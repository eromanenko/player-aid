## Setup
Arrange the 9 village tiles in a 3x3 grid. Place the 4 player boards around the village (matching the 4 colors). Each player takes a Taoist monk figure and places it on the center village tile. Give players their starting Qi (health) and Tao tokens.

## Gameplay
Ghost Stories is a cooperative game. Your goal is to defeat the incarnation(s) of Wu-Feng. Each player's turn has two phases:

1. **Yin Phase (Ghosts Act):**
   - Apply the effects of all ghosts currently on your board (e.g., Haunters move forward, Tormentors force a curse die roll).
   - If your board is full (3 ghosts are already there), you lose 1 Qi and skip drawing a new ghost.
   - If your board is NOT full, draw 1 ghost card and place it on the player board matching the ghost's color (or on your board if it's black).

2. **Yang Phase (You Act):**
   - **Move:** You may move your Taoist 1 tile orthogonally (up, down, left, or right).
   - **Action (choose ONE):**
     - *Exorcise:* Attempt to defeat a ghost adjacent to your tile. Roll the 3 Tao dice. You must match the ghost's resistance color/number. You can spend your Tao tokens of the matching color to supplement your roll.
     - *Village Help:* Use the special ability of the village tile you are standing on.

## Winning & Losing
**You Win:** If you defeat the final incarnation of Wu-Feng (who appears near the bottom of the ghost deck).
**You Lose:** If all Taoists die (reach 0 Qi), OR if 3 village tiles become haunted, OR if you must draw a ghost but the deck is empty.
