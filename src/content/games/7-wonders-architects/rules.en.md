## Setup
Each player takes a Wonder "box". Place all your Wonder stages "under construction" side up (order them from bottom to top). Shuffle your deck and place it **face up** between you and the player to your left.
In the center of the table, place:
1. The central deck **face down**.
2. Progress tokens (green) — 3 face up (4 if playing with 4+ players), the rest face down.
3. Conflict tokens (red/blue) — "Peace" (dove) side up.
4. The Cat pawn (gives 2 points and lets you peek at the central deck).

## Player Turn
On your turn, you must take exactly **1 card** from three available options:
1. The top card of the deck to your left (face up).
2. The top card of the deck to your right (face up).
3. The top card of the central deck (face down).

Place the chosen card in front of you.

## Card Types
* **Grey (Resources) & Yellow (Coins):** Used to build your Wonder. Coins act as "wildcard" resources.
* **Blue (Victory Points):** Give victory points at the end of the game. If the card has a Cat symbol, you immediately take the Cat pawn.
* **Green (Science):** Collect 2 identical symbols or 3 different symbols to immediately exchange them for one **Progress token** (face up or face down). These tokens provide unique bonuses.
* **Red (Military):** Provide military shields. If you draw a red card with **one or two horns**, you must flip that many "Peace" tokens to their "Battle" side in the center of the table.

## Building Your Wonder
As soon as you have the required combination of resources to build the next (lowest available) stage of your Wonder, you **must** build it at the end of your turn.
* Requirements (e.g., 2 different, 3 identical) are paid with resource/coin cards.
* Discard the used cards and flip the Wonder stage to its "built" side.

## Military Conflicts
As soon as all tokens in the center are flipped to "Battle", a conflict begins immediately:
1. Compare your total number of shields (on all your red cards) with your left neighbor, then with your right neighbor.
2. For each victory, take a Military Victory token (3 points).
3. After the battle, **all players must discard all red cards that have horns**. Red cards without horns are kept!
4. Flip the Battle tokens back to "Peace".

## End of the Game
The game ends **instantly** as soon as any player completes the final (highest) stage of their Wonder.
Tally your points from:
1. Built Wonder stages.
2. The Cat pawn (if you have it — 2 points).
3. Blue cards.
4. Military Victory tokens (3 points each).
5. Progress tokens (that award points).

The player with the most points wins!
