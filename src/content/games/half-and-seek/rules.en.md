## Setup
Place the small Object cards face down in a stack (or spread them out). Spread the large "Doodle" cards on the table. Each player gets a plastic mirror. 

## Gameplay
Half and Seek is a visual perception game. The large cards show abstract doodles that hide "halves" of various objects. 

1. **Reveal:** Turn over one small Object card so everyone can see what they are looking for.
2. **Search:** All players simultaneously look at the large Doodle cards on the table.
3. **Use the Mirror:** When you think you found the matching half, place your mirror vertically on that spot on the large card. The reflection in the mirror combined with the printed half should complete the image shown on the small Object card.
4. **Claim:** If you are correct, you claim the small Object card! (The large Doodle card remains on the table).
5. **Next Round:** Reveal the next small Object card and search again.

## Winning
The game continues until a player successfully collects 10 small Object cards. That player is the winner!
