## Game Variant 1

### Setup
Shuffle the large and small cards separately and place both decks face down on the table.
Each player takes a mirror. Also, each player takes 1 large card from the deck and places it face down in front of them.
Lay out 12 small cards from the deck face up in the center of the table.
Then, all players simultaneously flip their large cards face up and the game begins.

![Setup for Variant 1](/games/half-and-seek/var1.jpg)

### Gameplay
Everyone plays simultaneously. Each player uses their mirror to search their large card for halves of the objects depicted on the small cards in the center of the table.
As soon as a player finds an object, they show it to the other players using the mirror, then take the small card.
The player replaces it with a new small card from the deck, and the game continues.
> [!WARNING]
> If a player makes a mistake, they give up one of their small cards as a penalty — placing the card face down under the small card deck. If the player has no small cards, nothing happens.

Each player has the right to say "Swap!" once per game: then **ALL** players discard their large cards, take new large cards from the deck, and continue playing.

### End of Game
As soon as a player collects 10 small cards, the game ends, and that player is declared the winner.

## Game Variant 2

### Setup
Shuffle the large and small cards separately. Place the deck of large cards face down in the center of the table.
Lay out 12 small cards from the deck face up around it. Place the remaining deck of small cards face down nearby.
Each player takes a mirror.
Then, the players flip the entire deck of large cards face up and the game begins.

![Setup for Variant 2](/games/half-and-seek/var2.jpg)

### Gameplay
Everyone plays simultaneously. Without using a mirror, players search the large card in the center of the table for halves of the objects depicted on the small cards surrounding it.
As soon as a player finds an object, they show it to the other players using the mirror, then take the small card, and the game continues.
> [!WARNING]
> If a player makes a mistake, they give up one of their small cards as a penalty — placing the card face down under the small card deck. If the player has no small cards, nothing happens.

When a player takes another small card from the center of the table, leaving only 9 out of 12, they immediately lay out three new small cards from the deck, discard the top large card from the deck, and the game continues.

> [!NOTE]
> *Note:* If all players agree that the large card in the center of the table contains no objects depicted on the face-up small cards, discard the top large card from the deck and continue playing.

### End of Game
As soon as a player collects 10 small cards, the game ends, and that player is declared the winner.

## Game Variant 3 (Team play, for 3–6 players)

### Setup
Divide into two equal teams (if there is an odd number of players, one team will have one more player).
Each player takes a mirror.
Otherwise, the setup follows the rules for Game Variant 2.

### Gameplay
The game follows the rules for Game Variant 2 with one exception: players pool all their won small cards for their team's score.

### End of Game
The first team to collect 10 small cards wins.
