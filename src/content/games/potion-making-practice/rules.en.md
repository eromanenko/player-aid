## Setup
Shuffle the deck. Deal 4 cards to each player. Place 4 cards face up in the center of the table; this is the "Desk of Elements". The rest is the draw pile. 

## Gameplay
Every card has two parts: a simple **Element** at the bottom, and a **Recipe** (for a potion, creature, or talisman) at the top. 

On your turn, you must perform two actions in this order:
1. **Draw:** Draw 1 card from the deck.
2. **Play:** Play 1 card from your hand. You can play it in one of two ways:
   - **As an Element:** Place the card in the Desk of Elements. You score 1 point (only if this element is *not* already on the desk). 
   - **As a Recipe:** Show the card and take the required ingredients from the Desk of Elements to build it. Place the completed recipe in front of you. You score points equal to the recipe's value. 
     - *Complex Recipes:* Advanced recipes might require a simple potion you or another player has already built! If you use another player's potion, *they* score half the points of your new creation!

**Spells:** Some cards act as Spells instead of Elements/Recipes. You can play a Spell to alter the Desk, steal creations, etc.

## Winning
The game ends when the draw deck is empty and players have played all their cards. The player with the most points wins!
