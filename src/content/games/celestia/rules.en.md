## Setup
Place the 9 City tiles in order (1 to 25). Place the corresponding treasure decks next to each city. All players place their pawns on the airship on the first city. Deal starting equipment cards to each player.

## Gameplay
Each journey, one player acts as the Captain, and the rest are Passengers.
1. **Roll Dice:** The Captain rolls dice equal to the number of hazard symbols shown on the *next* city.
2. **Passengers Decide:** In clockwise order, each passenger decides to **Stay** on the airship OR **Disembark**. If you disembark, you take 1 treasure card from the *current* city and are safe for the rest of the journey.
3. **Overcome Hazards:** The Captain MUST play equipment cards from their hand matching the rolled hazards (e.g., compass for fog, cannon for pirates).
   - *Success:* The airship moves to the next city.
   - *Crash:* If the Captain cannot beat all hazards, the ship crashes. Everyone still on board gets NOTHING. A new journey begins from the first city.

<div data-expansion="celestia-a-little-initiative">

**A Little Initiative (Expansion):**
When deciding to stay or disembark, you can play a **Rowboat** card to leave the main airship and travel alone! The rowboat has room for only 1 player (first to play the card). If you take the rowboat, you must roll your own dice and face hazards entirely by yourself on your turn.
</div>

## Winning
The game ends immediately when a player reaches 50 or more points in collected treasures. The player with the highest total value wins!
