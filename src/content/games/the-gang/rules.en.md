## Setup
The Gang is a cooperative version of Texas Hold'em Poker. Each player gets a ranking token (1 is the lowest hand, up to the number of players for the highest). The goal is to successfully rob a certain number of vaults by perfectly ranking your hands.

## Gameplay
Each round represents an attempt to rob a vault. A round has four phases, mirroring standard Texas Hold'em:

1. **Pre-Flop:** Each player is dealt 2 private cards. Players secretly estimate the strength of their potential poker hand and take a ranking token from the center (e.g., in a 4-player game, 1 is the worst hand, 4 is the best).
2. **The Flop:** 3 community cards are revealed. Players may now swap their ranking tokens with each other if they think their hand strength has changed relative to the team.
3. **The Turn:** 1 more community card is revealed. Players may swap tokens again.
4. **The River:** The final community card is revealed. Players have one last chance to swap tokens.

**Communication:** You MAY NOT talk about your specific cards or the exact probability of your hand! You can only use vague terms or rely on the ranking tokens you claim.

## Winning the Round
After the River, all players reveal their private cards. Determine everyone's best 5-card poker hand using their 2 private cards and the 5 community cards. 
- If the ranking tokens perfectly match the actual strength of the hands (1 holds the worst hand, the highest token holds the best), the team successfully robs the vault!
- If the tokens do NOT match, the team triggers the alarm. Trigger too many alarms, and you lose the game!
