## Setup
Players split into 2 teams (Red and Blue). Each team chooses one Spymaster. The Spymasters sit on the opposite side of the table.
Layout a **5x4** grid of picture cards. The Spymasters draw a random Key card (designed for the 5x4 grid) and place it so only they can see it. The Key card shows which pictures belong to which team, neutral pictures, and the Assassin (black square).

## Gameplay
Teams take turns. On your team's turn:
1. **The Clue:** The Spymaster gives exactly **one word** and **one number** (e.g., "Space 2"). The word must relate to the pictures belonging to your team on the table, and the number tells how many pictures relate to that clue.
2. **Guessing:** The operatives discuss and touch a picture card to guess it.
   - *Correct:* If it's your team's picture, cover it with your color. You may guess again (up to the clue's number + 1 guesses total).
   - *Neutral:* If it's a neutral picture, cover it with an innocent bystander. **Your turn ends immediately.**
   - *Enemy:* If it's the other team's picture, cover it with their color. **Your turn ends immediately.**
   - *Assassin:* If you touch the Assassin picture, **your team loses the game immediately!**

## Winning
The game ends when one team covers all their pictures. That team wins! (Alternatively, the other team wins if someone touches the Assassin).
