## Setup
Sort the development cards by level (I, II, III) into 3 decks. Reveal 4 cards from each deck face up. Shuffle the Noble tiles and reveal a number of tiles equal to the number of players plus 1. Sort the gem tokens by color into stacks. 

## Gameplay
On your turn, you must perform exactly ONE of these four actions:

1. **Take 3 gem tokens of different colors.**
2. **Take 2 gem tokens of the same color.** 
   - *Rule:* You can only do this if there are at least 4 tokens in that stack *before* you take them.
   - *Hand Limit:* You can never have more than 10 tokens at the end of your turn. Discard extras.
3. **Reserve 1 development card and take 1 Gold token (joker).**
   - Take a card from the table or the top of a deck. Keep it hidden. You can only have up to 3 reserved cards. (You get the gold even if you just reserve).
4. **Purchase 1 face-up development card from the table or your reserved cards.**
   - Spend the required gem tokens to buy the card. Gold tokens count as any color.
   - **Discounts:** Every card you buy gives you a permanent gem bonus of a specific color, reducing the cost of future cards by 1 token per bonus!

**Nobles:**
At the end of your turn, if your permanent gem bonuses (from purchased cards, not tokens) match the requirements of a Noble tile, you receive a visit from that Noble! Take the tile. (Only 1 Noble per turn).

## Winning
The game end is triggered when a player reaches **15 Prestige Points** (summed from development cards and Nobles). Finish the current round so everyone gets an equal number of turns. The player with the most points wins!
