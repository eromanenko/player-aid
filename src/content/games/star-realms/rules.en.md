## Setup
Each player starts with **50 Authority** (health) and a personal deck of 10 ships (8 Scouts, 2 Vipers). Shuffle the trade deck and reveal 5 cards in the center row. Place the Explorer stack next to them.

## Gameplay
Star Realms is a fast-paced deck-building game of space combat. 

On your turn, you may perform the following actions in any order:
1. **Play Cards:** Play ships and bases from your hand. They generate Trade (gold coin), Combat (red target), or Authority (green shield).
   - *Bases* stay in play until destroyed. *Outposts* (black shield) must be destroyed by your opponent before they can attack you or your non-outpost bases!
   - *Ally Abilities:* If you play two or more cards of the same faction (color) in a single turn, you trigger their powerful Ally abilities.
2. **Use Trade:** Buy cards from the center row or Explorer stack. Purchased cards go to your **discard pile**.
3. **Use Combat:** Attack your opponent or their bases. (1 Combat = 1 Damage).
4. **Scrap (Trash icon):** Some cards allow you to scrap a card from your hand or discard pile, permanently removing it from the game to thin your deck.

**End of Turn:**
Discard any remaining cards in your hand, all ships you played this turn, and any unused Trade/Combat points. Draw a new hand of 5 cards.

## Winning
Reduce your opponent's Authority to zero!

## Frontiers (Standalone Expansion)
Frontiers can be played as a standalone game for 1-4 players, or combined with the base game to support up to 6 players. 

**New Mechanics:**
- **Double-Ally Abilities:** Some cards have a double-faction icon. You must play TWO other cards of that faction to trigger this massive bonus!
- **Solo & Co-op Bosses:** Frontiers includes large oversized Boss cards. You and your friends can team up to fight automated, challenging Bosses. Each Boss has its own specific rules and difficulty levels printed on its card.

## Gambit Set (Expansion)
The Gambit expansion adds secret, powerful abilities that you get at the start of the game!

**Setup:** 
Shuffle the Gambit cards and deal a specific number (usually 1 or 2) face down to each player. 

**Using Gambits:**
- At the start of the game, players reveal their Gambits.
- Gambits are NOT part of your deck. They stay in front of you.
- During your Main Phase, you may scrap a Gambit to use its ability (e.g., gain 3 Trade, deal 4 Damage, or draw a card). 
- Some Gambits provide a passive effect as long as they are face up. Once scrapped, they are gone for the rest of the game!
