## Setup
Build the Complex by shuffling the room tiles and creating a 5x5 grid face down, with the Central Room in the middle and Room 25 hidden somewhere. Each player chooses a character, takes their action tokens, and a reminder sheet. 

## Gameplay
Room 25 Ultimate is a survival game. You must find Room 25 and escape the complex before the countdown reaches zero! The game can be played cooperatively or with hidden Guards trying to stop the prisoners.

Each round has three phases:
1. **Programming:** Every player secretly chooses up to 2 action tokens and places them face down in order. (Moves cannot be discussed in specific detail).
2. **Actions:** Going in turn order, players reveal their first action token and resolve it. Then, everyone resolves their second token.
   - *Move:* Move to an adjacent room (flip it face up if it was hidden). Beware of deadly traps!
   - *Look:* Secretly peek at an adjacent face-down room.
   - *Push:* Push another character in your room into an adjacent room!
   - *Control:* Shift an entire row or column of the complex by one space.
3. **Countdown:** Advance the turn marker. If time runs out, everyone trapped loses!

## Winning
**Cooperative Mode:** All prisoners must find Room 25, gather inside it, and use the Control action to slide it out of the complex before the countdown ends.
**Suspicion Mode:** The Prisoners must escape. The Guards win if they prevent the escape or eliminate two Prisoners.
