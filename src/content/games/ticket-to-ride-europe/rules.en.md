## Setup
Give each player 45 trains, 3 stations, and a scoring marker. Deal 4 Train Car cards to each player. Deal 1 Long Destination Ticket and 3 regular Destination Tickets to each player; everyone must keep at least 2 tickets (discard unkept ones). Reveal 5 Train Car cards face-up.

## Gameplay
On your turn, you must perform exactly ONE of these four actions:

1. **Draw Train Car Cards:** Draw 2 cards (either from the face-up display or blind from the deck). *Exception:* If you take a face-up Locomotive (wild card), you can only draw 1 card total this turn.
2. **Claim a Route:** Play a set of cards matching the color and length of a route on the board. Place your plastic trains on it and score points immediately.
   - *Ferries:* Require a specific number of Locomotive cards (indicated by locomotive icons on the spaces).
   - *Tunnels:* Play the required cards. Then, reveal the top 3 cards from the draw pile. For each revealed card that matches the color you used (or is a Locomotive), you MUST pay one additional matching card from your hand! If you can't or won't, you take your cards back and your turn ends.
3. **Draw Destination Tickets:** Draw 3 tickets from the top of the deck. You must keep at least 1 of them.
4. **Build a Train Station:** A station allows you to use ONE opponent's route connecting to that city to complete your tickets at the end of the game. Cost: 1 card of any color for your first station, 2 matching cards for your second, and 3 matching cards for your third.

## Winning
The end game is triggered when a player's stock of colored trains gets down to **2 or fewer** at the end of their turn. Each player (including that player) takes one final turn.
Calculate final scores:
- Add points for completed Destination Tickets.
- Subtract points for uncompleted Destination Tickets.
- Add 4 points for each unused Train Station.
- Add 10 points to the player(s) with the longest continuous path (European Express Bonus).
The player with the most points wins!
