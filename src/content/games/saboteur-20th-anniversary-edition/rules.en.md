## Setup
Place the Start card and the 3 Goal cards (face down, one contains the gold) on the table with exactly 7 card widths between them. Deal Dwarf role cards secretly (Miners vs. Saboteurs). Deal starting hands.

## Gameplay
On your turn, you MUST play 1 card, then draw 1 card.
You can play a card in three ways:
1. **Path Card:** Add to the maze. It must connect legally to the Start card's path. Miners want to reach the gold; Saboteurs want to block the path!
2. **Action Card:** 
   - *Break a tool:* Place in front of a player. They cannot play path cards until it's fixed!
   - *Fix a tool:* Remove a broken tool card.
   - *Rockfall:* Remove any path card from the maze (except Start/Goals).
   - *Map:* Secretly peek at one of the Goal cards.
3. **Pass:** Discard 1 card face down to the discard pile.

## Winning a Round
The round ends when players reach the Goal card with the Gold (Miners win!), or when the deck runs out and everyone passes (Saboteurs win!).
- *Miners win:* They draw Gold piece cards and distribute them (starting with the player who reached the gold, passing counter-clockwise).
- *Saboteurs win:* They get a fixed amount of gold depending on the number of Saboteurs.

## 20th Anniversary Additions
This edition includes new Action cards, new Path cards (like toll bridges or doors), and new Roles (like the Boss, Profiteer, or Geologist) which have unique ways to score gold, changing the dynamic from simple team vs. team. 

Play 3 rounds. The player with the most gold pieces at the end wins!
