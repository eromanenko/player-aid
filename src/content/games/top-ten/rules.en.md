## Setup
Top Ten is a cooperative party game. Place the mat in the center with 5 Unicorn tokens on the green zone and 0 Poop tokens on the red zone. Deal one number card (1 to 10) face down to each player. One player is chosen as the first Capten (Captain). 

## Gameplay
The goal is to survive 5 rounds without turning all your Unicorns into Poop.

Each round follows these steps:
1. **Read the Theme:** The Capten draws a theme card and reads one of the scenarios out loud. (e.g., "A bad excuse for being late to work. From least believable to most believable").
2. **Secret Numbers:** Each player looks at their secret number card (1 is the lowest intensity/least, 10 is the highest intensity/most).
3. **Give Answers:** Starting from the left of the Capten, each player invents and says an answer that matches the intensity of their secret number. The Capten also answers!
4. **Guess the Order:** The Capten must now guess the order of the answers from **lowest to highest**. They point to players one by one.
5. **Reveal:** When the Capten points to a player, that player reveals their number card. 
   - **Mistake!** If a revealed card is LOWER than the previously revealed card, the team loses a token! Move one token from the Unicorn zone to the Poop zone.

*Note:* After the round, pass the Capten role to the next player, shuffle all number cards, and deal new ones.

## Winning
If the team survives 5 rounds with at least one Unicorn token remaining in the green zone, you win! If all tokens turn into Poop, you lose immediately.
