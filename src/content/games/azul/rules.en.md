## Setup
Place the Factory displays in a circle in the center of the table: **5** for 2 players, **7** for 3 players, **9** for 4 players.
Place exactly **4 tiles** drawn from the bag onto each Factory display.
Place the starting player marker in the center of the table.

## Gameplay
The game is played over multiple rounds, each consisting of 3 phases:

### Phase 1: Factory Offers
On your turn, you **must** pick tiles in one of two ways:
1. Take **all tiles of the same color** from any one Factory display. Push the remaining unchosen tiles from that display to the center of the table.
2. Take **all tiles of the same color** from the center of the table. If you are the first player in this round to take from the center, also take the starting player marker and place it in your floor line.

After picking up the tiles, you must add them to one of the 5 pattern lines on the left side of your player board.
* You can only add tiles to an empty pattern line, or to a line that already contains tiles of the **exact same color**.
* If you pick up more tiles than the pattern line can hold, the "excess" tiles must be placed in the floor line at the bottom of your board (these will deduct points).

This phase ends when all tiles have been taken from both the Factory displays and the center.

### Phase 2: Wall Tiling
Players can perform this phase simultaneously. Go through your 5 pattern lines from top to bottom:
1. If a line is **completely filled**, move the rightmost tile of that line to the corresponding color space on the wall in the same row.
2. Score points immediately (see Scoring below).
3. Discard the remaining tiles from that filled pattern line into the box lid.
4. If a line is **not completely filled**, it stays exactly as it is for the next round.

**Scoring a placed tile:**
* **1 point** if there are no tiles directly adjacent (horizontally or vertically) to the newly placed tile.
* If there are horizontal adjacent tiles, count the number of linked tiles in that row (including the new one) and score that many points.
* If there are vertical adjacent tiles, count the number of linked tiles in that column (including the new one) and score that many points.
*(A tile can be counted twice if it links both horizontally and vertically).*

At the end of this phase, deduct points for all tiles and the starting player marker in your floor line. Then discard them into the box lid (keep the starting marker in front of you).

### Phase 3: Preparing the Next Round
If nobody has completed a horizontal line of 5 tiles on their wall, refill each Factory display with 4 tiles from the bag and start a new round.

## End of Game
The game ends immediately after the Wall Tiling phase in which at least one player completes **a full horizontal line of 5 tiles** on their wall.

**End Game Bonuses:**
* **+2 points** for each completed horizontal line.
* **+7 points** for each completed vertical line.
* **+10 points** for each color of which you have placed all 5 tiles on your wall.

The player with the most points wins! (Tie-breaker: most completed horizontal lines).
