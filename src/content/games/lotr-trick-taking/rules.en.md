## Setup
The Fellowship of the Ring: Trick-Taking Game is a cooperative campaign game. Choose a chapter to play (from 1 to 18). Draft character cards as specified by the chapter. Each character has a unique, hidden or public objective they MUST complete. Shuffle the playing deck and deal cards to players.

## Gameplay
The game is played in a series of tricks. Like standard trick-taking games:
- The lead player plays a card.
- Other players **must follow suit** if they can. If not, they can play any card.
- The highest number of the lead suit wins the trick.

**The Ring Suit:** 
There is a special "Ring" suit. You **cannot** lead a trick with a Ring card unless someone has already played a Ring card in a previous trick (tracked by flipping the Ring token). The "1 of Rings" is a powerful card that can win any trick!

**Communication:**
Players cannot tell each other what cards they hold, unless an effect or specific chapter rule allows limited communication.

## Winning & Losing
**Winning:** At the end of the round, check the objectives of all drafted characters. If *every single player* successfully completed their character's objective, the team wins the chapter!
**Losing:** If even one player fails their objective, the team loses the chapter and must try it again.
