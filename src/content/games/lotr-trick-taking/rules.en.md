## Setup (for Chapter I)
1. Take the Chapter I card "A Long-expected Party" and place it face up in the center of the table along with the indicated character cards.
2. Place a star token on each character card marked with a "*" on the chapter card.
3. Shuffle the main deck and place the top card face up near the chapter card. This is the **lost card**. If you reveal the *1 of Rings*, reveal the next card to be the lost card, and shuffle the *1 of Rings* back into the deck.
4. Deal the remaining cards of the main deck to the players depending on the player count. *(Players look at their cards but keep them hidden from others)*:
   - **2 players:** 12 cards each.
   - **3 players:** 12 cards each.
   - **4 players:** 9 cards each.
5. The player who was dealt the *1 of Rings* takes the Frodo card and the Ring token with the "Cannot lead with rings" side face up. The player keeps the *1 of Rings* in their hand. Use the side of the Frodo card that matches the player count (3 players or 4 players).
6. Starting with the player to Frodo's left, players take turns choosing a character card. In this round, you must take the characters with star tokens (remove the star tokens after choosing). *Tip: When choosing character cards, carefully study your hand and consider which character's task will be easiest for you to complete.*
7. Starting with the player to Frodo's left, each player resolves their character's setup steps (marked with a hammer symbol).
   > [!NOTE]
   > **"Trade"** means the active character passes 1 card face down to one of the indicated characters. After looking at the received card, that character adds it to their hand and passes any 1 card face down in return (they may return the card they just received). You cannot trade with characters not currently in the game. Frodo cannot trade their *1 of Rings* card.
8. You are now ready to begin the game.

## Gameplay
The player who starts the trick (lead player) plays any card from their hand to the center of the table. The suit of this first card is the lead suit of the trick (hills, mountains, forests, shadows, or rings). **Frodo leads the first trick.**
Tricks are played using the standard "must follow suit" rule. Players must follow suit if they can. The trick is won by the highest card of the lead suit. The player leading the trick can play a card of any suit except rings.

The rings suit differs from the other suits:
* Ring cards have values from 1 to 5 (not 1 to 8 like the cards of the other 4 suits).
* A player cannot lead a trick with a ring card until any ring card has been played in a previous trick (if a player did not have cards of the lead suit and therefore played a ring card, or had only ring cards in hand).
* Only the 1 of rings card can win any trick (like a trump card in other trick-taking games). The 1 of rings can also be played as a normal, not trump card.
* Ring cards from 2 to 5 are considered normal (non-trump) cards.

The round ends after each player plays all their cards. Some characters may have a card left in their hand at the end of the round (such as Gandalf).
To win, all players must complete their characters' tasks at the end of the round.


**Communication:**
During setup and throughout the game, you may only discuss information that is available to all players. You may not show or discuss the cards in your hand or the hands of other players. During setup, you should not discuss the advantages of any particular character, as doing so might reveal certain information. Players may remind each other of their characters' tasks and special scenario rules, as well as the current state of their own tasks or any other public information (such as face-up cards on the table).

## End of the Chapter
After all players have played all the cards from their hands (except for Gandalf, who will have 1 card left), the round ends.

*   **Victory:** If every player completed their character's task in this round, you win together and complete the chapter. Set aside the card for this chapter and take the card for the next chapter from the box, along with any other necessary cards.
*   **Defeat:** If at least 1 player failed to complete their character's task, all players suffer defeat. In this case, play this chapter again to achieve victory.
