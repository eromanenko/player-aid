*Note: This is an expansion for Room 25. It introduces a purely cooperative puzzle-solving mode.*

## Setup
Choose one of the included Escape Room scenarios. Set up the complex according to the scenario book (some rooms will be placed face up, others face down in specific patterns). Deal the puzzle cards to the side. Players play cooperatively against the clock.

## Gameplay
The core actions (Move, Look, Push, Control) work exactly like the base game, but there are no hidden Guards. Instead, you must solve puzzles to unlock the complex!

**Puzzle Rooms & Secret Codes:**
- When you enter certain new rooms, you must draw a Puzzle Card or solve a riddle to proceed.
- You will need to find clues scattered across different rooms in the complex to decipher "Secret Codes".
- The game requires players to communicate, share clues they find, and logic out the solutions to unlock doors and activate special rooms.

**Scenario Objectives:**
Each scenario has specific win conditions detailed in its booklet. You are generally racing against a strict turn limit (the countdown track) to solve all the puzzles and escape.

## Winning
All players must survive, complete the scenario's specific puzzle objectives, and escape the complex before the countdown marker reaches zero. If time runs out, or if a player dies, everyone loses!
