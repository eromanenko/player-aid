## Setup
Deal 3 cards to each player. Shuffle the three letter cards ("G", "U", "B") randomly into the deck. Leave room in the center for a draw pile and a discard pile.

## Gameplay
GUBS is a card game where you try to build the biggest, safest colony of Gubs. On your turn:
1. **Draw:** Draw 1 card from the deck.
2. **Play:** Play as many cards from your hand as you want (you can also play 0).
   - *Free Gubs:* Place them face-up in front of you.
   - *Tools/Attachments:* Play tools (like spears, shields, mushroom rings) on your Gubs to protect them.
   - *Traps/Hazards:* Play cards to steal or destroy opponents' Gubs, or use events that affect the whole table.
3. **Discard:** If you have more than 8 cards in your hand, discard down to 8.

## Winning
The game ends *instantly* the moment the third letter card (completing the word G-U-B) is drawn from the deck! The player who has the most Free and Protected Gubs in their play area at that exact moment wins the game!
