## Setup
1. Deal one Gub card to each player (leave the Esteemed Elder Gub card in the deck). Players place their Gubs face up in front of them on the table.
2. Distribute the letter cards "G", "U", "B" evenly throughout the deck: place one card in the top third, the second in the middle, and the third in the bottom third of the deck. Thoroughly shuffle the deck.
3. Deal 3 cards to each player. If any of these are Event cards (with a lightning bolt symbol), shuffle them back into the deck and draw new ones. Keep your cards in your hand, hidden from other players.
4. Place the deck face down in the center of the table, leaving room next to it for a discard pile.

## Gameplay
The person who suggested playing chooses the first player, then play proceeds clockwise. On your turn, you may perform the following actions:
1. **DRAW:** Draw a card from the deck. You may choose not to draw a new card (sometimes this is justified), but you cannot skip this action two turns in a row. You can only do this at the beginning of your turn before you start playing cards.
2. **PLAY:** Play as many cards from your hand as you see fit. This action is not mandatory; sometimes you simply cannot play any cards from your hand. Read the text on the cards to learn what they do.
3. **DISCARD:** At the end of your turn, you must have no more than 8 cards in your hand. If you have more, discard the excess cards.
4. Pass the turn to the next player by saying "Your turn!".

## End of Game
The game ends immediately when a player draws the third letter card (G, U, or B) from the deck. The winner is the player who has managed to collect the most Free or Protected Gubs on their table. Gubs caught in a Trap and Gubs in a player's hand do not count.
In the event of a tie, the winner is the player who has the Esteemed Elder Gub card face up in front of them. If no one has this card, the player with the fewest cards in hand wins.
