## Setup
Players split into 2 teams (Red and Blue). Each team chooses one Spymaster. The Spymasters sit on the opposite side of the table from their teams.
Layout a 5x5 grid of word cards. The Spymasters draw a random Key card and place it so only they can see it. The Key card shows which words belong to which team, neutral words, and the Assassin (black square).

## Gameplay
Teams take turns. On your team's turn:
1. **The Clue:** The Spymaster gives exactly **one word** and **one number** (e.g., "Animal 3"). The word must relate to your team's words on the table, and the number tells how many words relate to that clue.
2. **Guessing:** The operatives discuss and touch a card to guess it.
   - *Correct:* If it's your team's word, cover it with your color. You may guess again (you can make up to the clue's number + 1 guesses total).
   - *Neutral:* If it's a neutral word, cover it with an innocent bystander. **Your turn ends immediately.**
   - *Enemy:* If it's the other team's word, cover it with their color. **Your turn ends immediately.**
   - *Assassin:* If you touch the Assassin card, **your team loses the game immediately!**

## Winning
The game ends when one team covers all their words. That team wins! (Alternatively, the other team wins if someone touches the Assassin).
