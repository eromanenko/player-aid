## Setup
Each player gets a boat board. Place the island board in the center with Oshax (black cats) and common treasures. Fill the fields left and right of the island with cat tiles randomly drawn from the bag (2 cats per player per field).

## Gameplay
The game is played over 5 days (rounds). Each day has 5 phases:

1. **Fishing:** Each player takes 20 Fish tokens.
2. **Explore (Drafting):** 
   - Deal 7 cards to each player. 
   - Draft cards (keep 2, pass the rest) until everyone has 7 drafted cards.
   - You must **pay fish** for the cards you want to keep! Discard any you don't pay for.
3. **Read Lessons:** Players play "Lesson" cards (blue) face down. These are secret end-game scoring goals! Public lessons go face-up.
4. **Rescue Cats:** 
   - Players simultaneously reveal "Rescue" cards (green) to determine turn order (most boots goes first).
   - In turn order, you may rescue a cat from the fields by paying its fish cost (left field = 3 fish, right field = 5 fish) AND spending a basket (from cards or permanent). 
   - Place the cat polyomino on your boat immediately.
5. **Rare Finds:** Play yellow/brown cards to get Oshax (wild color cats) or Treasures. Treasures fill gaps on your boat!

**Placement Rules:**
- The first tile can go anywhere. Every subsequent tile MUST touch an existing tile on your boat.
- You can't move a tile once placed. You can't overlap tiles or go off the boat.

## Winning
After 5 days, calculate scores:
- **-1 point** for each visible rat on your boat.
- **-5 points** for each unfilled room on your boat.
- Points for **Cat Families** (groups of 3+ touching cats of the same color).
- Points from your completed **Lesson cards**.
The player with the most points wins!
