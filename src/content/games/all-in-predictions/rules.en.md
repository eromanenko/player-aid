## Setup

1. Deal 5 cards, 1 prediction token, and a combination reference card (with the same side up) to each player.
2. Assemble the board depending on the number of players:
   - **2 players** - 4 slots
   - **3 players** - 5 slots
   - **4+ players** - 6 slots

   *The slots will be filled with cards from left to right in 2 rows. A card from the bottom row cannot be taken if it is covered by a card in the second row.*
3. Place 2 cards from the deck onto the board.
4. Add to the rune pot for the rounds:

| Round | 2 players | 3+ players |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| 1 | 2 | 4 |
| 2 | 4 | 6 |
| 3 | 6 | 8 |

## Player's Turn

On their turn, a player takes one of 3 actions:
- **Play a card** from their hand onto the board, resolving the card's effects. Mandatory effects are marked with `✧`.
- **Skip a turn** by placing a card from the deck onto the board.
- **Go all-in** by placing their cards face down on the table and adding 2 cards from the deck onto the board.

*At the end of the turn, a player must have 5 cards left in their hand. Any excess cards are discarded.*

## End of Round and Predictions

The game consists of **3 rounds**, each ending when the board is filled with 2 rows at the end of a player's turn. After each round, the **prediction phase** begins.

Each player, starting with the one whose turn would have been next, places their token next to the player who they believe has the highest combination.

- Everyone who guessed correctly splits the round's pot evenly. The remainder goes to the next round's pot.
- Each player receives an additional number of runes corresponding to the rank of their hand.
- Rune cards grant an additional 2 runes each.
