## Setup
Take Time is a cooperative puzzle game containing 40 "clocks" (levels). Open the envelope for your current chapter. Place the clock board and set the hands as instructed. Deal the 12 Solar (1-12) and 12 Lunar (1-12) cards among the players.

## Gameplay
Your team must complete the current puzzle by placing cards face-down into the segments around the clock face.

A round has three phases:
1. **Discussion:** Look at the current clock's unique rules. Discuss your strategy, but keep the exact numbers in your hand secret!
2. **Placement:** Communication is now FORBIDDEN. Players take turns placing their cards face-down into the clock segments. 
   - *Universal Rule:* Unless overridden by the specific puzzle, the total value of cards in each clock segment must be **greater than or equal to** the previous segment (clockwise), and no segment's total can exceed 24.
3. **Resolution:** Reveal all placed cards. Check if your card placement satisfies both the universal rules and the specific rules of the current clock puzzle.

## Winning (or Failing)
- **Success:** If you met all conditions, you win the level! Move on to the next puzzle.
- **Failure:** If any condition is broken, you fail. You can immediately retry the level or put it away for later.
