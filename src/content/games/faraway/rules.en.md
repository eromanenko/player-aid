## Setup
Shuffle the Region deck (square cards) and deal 3 to each player. Reveal a central market of face-up Region cards equal to the number of players + 1. Give each player a random Sanctuary card.

## Gameplay
The game lasts exactly 8 rounds. In each round:
1. **Play a Card:** All players secretly choose one Region card from their hand and reveal it simultaneously. Place it to the right of your previously played cards (building a row from left to right).
2. **Draft a Card:** The player who played the card with the *lowest* number gets to draft a new card from the center first. The highest number drafts last.
3. **Refill Market:** Add 1 new card to the center so there are enough for the next round.
4. **Sanctuaries:** If the card you played has a higher number than your previous card, you discover a Sanctuary! Draw a Sanctuary card (plus as many Sanctuaries as you have map symbols on your previously played cards) and place them in your play area.

## Winning (The Twist!)
At the end of the 8th round, you score your row of 8 cards in **REVERSE ORDER** (from right to left).
- You flip all cards face down.
- Reveal the rightmost card. It only scores if you meet its requirements using *only* the resources visible on that card, previously revealed cards (to its right), and your Sanctuaries.
- Continue revealing and scoring from right to left.
- The player with the most points wins!
