## Setup
Shuffle the deck (containing Merchant Ships, Pirate Ships, Pirate Captains, and 1 Admiral). Deal 5 cards to each player. 

## Gameplay
On your turn, you must perform exactly ONE of two actions:
1. **Draw a Card:** Draw the top card of the deck into your hand.
2. **Play a Card:**
   - *Merchant Ship:* Play a merchant ship face up in front of you. It's worth coins, but it's vulnerable!
   - *Pirate Ship:* Play a pirate ship to attack ANY merchant ship on the table (even your own). If it's the first pirate attacking that merchant, choose any color. If other pirates are already attacking it, you must play a *different* color than the ones already there.
   - *Pirate Captain:* Play a captain of a specific color to automatically beat all other pirate ships of that color.
   - *Admiral:* Can only be played by the player who originally played the merchant ship. Beats all pirates!

*Capturing:* At the *start* of your turn, check the merchant ships you are attacking. If your attacking pirate ships (or captain) have the highest total skull value of all attackers on that merchant ship, you capture it! Place it in your score pile. Discard all attacking pirate ships.

## Winning
The game ends when the deck is empty and one player plays their last card. Sum the coins on your captured merchant ships, and subtract the coins on any merchant ships left in your hand. The player with the highest score wins!
