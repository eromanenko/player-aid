## Setup
Place the board (the Periodic Table). Set out Goal Cards and the Academic Track. Each player gets a microscope token (placed on an element), a set of research cubes, and an Erlenmeyer flask token for the Academic Track.

## Gameplay
On your turn, you must spend Energy tokens to move your microscope across the Periodic Table. 

**Movement (Periodic Trends):**
You pay energy to select trends (like "Increase Atomic Number", "Decrease Atomic Mass", etc.) to move your microscope up, down, left, or right. 
- You can activate multiple trends in one turn by spending more energy.
- Alternatively, you can take all the energy tokens sitting on one trend to activate *only* that trend.

**Researching:**
When your microscope lands on an element that is required by a face-up Goal Card, you can place one of your research cubes on that card. 

**Academic Track:**
Certain movements allow you to advance your flask on the Academic Track, which gives you immediate bonuses and endgame points.

## Winning
When a Goal Card has all its elements researched, the players who contributed cubes score points. The game triggers its end when a certain number of Goal Card stacks are depleted, or someone reaches the end of the Academic Track. Add up points from completed Goals, the Academic Track, and remaining energy/objectives. The player with the most points wins!
