## Setup
Shuffle the deck of cards with the "Date" side facing down. Deal 4 cards to each player (keep the date hidden!). Place the rest of the deck in the center. Draw one card from the top of the deck and place it in the center, "Date" side UP. This is the start of the timeline.

## Gameplay
Your goal is to be the first player to get rid of all your cards.

On your turn:
1. **Choose a Card:** Pick one card from your hand.
2. **Place It:** Place the card in the timeline where you think it belongs chronologically. 
   - You can place it before the first card, after the last card, or between any two cards in the timeline.
3. **Reveal the Date:** Flip the card over to reveal the date.
   - **Correct!** If the card was placed in the correct chronological order, it stays in the timeline. Your turn is over.
   - **Incorrect!** If the card is in the wrong place, it is discarded. You must draw a new card from the deck to replace it.

*Note:* If you think your card has the exact same date as a card already in the timeline, you can place it adjacently (either before or after the card with the same date).

## Winning
If you are the only player in a round to correctly place your last card, you win!
If multiple players correctly place their last card in the same round, they each draw 1 more card and continue playing sudden-death until only one player gets it right.
