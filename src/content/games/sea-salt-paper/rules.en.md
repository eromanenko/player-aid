## Setup
Shuffle the deck. Leave room for two discard piles. A random player goes first.

## Gameplay
On your turn, you must take a card in one of two ways:
1. **Blind Draw:** Draw the top 2 cards from the deck, keep 1, and place the other face up in one of the two discard piles.
2. **From Discard:** Take the top card from either discard pile (if available).

**Playing Effects (Duos):**
After taking a card, you may play a pair of cards (a duo) from your hand to apply an effect:
* 🦀 **2 Crabs:** Take a card from any discard pile (you cannot search the pile, only the top).
* ⛵ **2 Boats:** Take another turn.
* 🐟 **2 Fish:** Draw the top card from the deck to your hand.
* 🦈 **Shark + Swimmer:** Steal a random card from another player's hand.

## End of Round
If at the end of your turn you have **7 or more points** (points in hand + played cards), you must declare the end of the round in one of two ways:

1. **"STOP"**: The round ends immediately. All players simply score their points.
2. **"LAST CHANCE"**: You bet on your victory. Other players take one more turn, then:
   * **If you still have the most points:** You score your regular points + color bonus (1 point per card of your most abundant color). Other players score *only* their color bonus.
   * **If someone beats you:** You score *only* your color bonus. All other players score their regular points.

## Winning
The game ends when someone reaches:
* **40 points** (for 2 players)
* **35 points** (for 3 players)
* **30 points** (for 4 players)

*(Epic Win: A player who collects all 4 Mermaid cards instantly wins the entire game, regardless of points).*
