## Setup
Shuffle the deck. Leave room for two discard piles next to the deck. Choose a random starting player.

## Gameplay
On your turn, you must draw a card in one of two ways:
1. **Draw from deck:** Draw the top 2 cards from the deck, keep 1 in your hand, and place the other face up on one of the two discard piles.
2. **Draw from discard:** Draw the top card from either of the two discard piles (if available).

**Playing Effects (Duos):**
After drawing, you may play a pair of cards (a duo) from your hand face up in front of you to apply its effect:
* 🦀 **2 Crabs:** Take an available card of your choice from either discard pile (you cannot look through the pile).
* ⛵ **2 Boats:** Take another turn immediately.
* 🐟 **2 Fish:** Draw the top card from the deck into your hand.
* 🦈 **Shark + Swimmer:** Steal a random card from another player's hand.

## End of the Round
If at the end of your turn you have **7 or more points** (points in hand + played cards), you must declare the end of the round in one of two ways:

1. **"STOP"**: The round ends immediately. All players count their points.
2. **"LAST CHANCE"**: You bet that you will have the most points. All other players take one final turn, then:
   * **If you still have the most points:** You score your regular points + a color bonus (1 point per card of your most abundant color). All other players score *only* their color bonus.
   * **If someone beats you:** You score *only* your color bonus. All other players score their regular points.

## Winning the Game
The game ends when a player reaches the point threshold across multiple rounds:
* **40 points** (2 players)
* **35 points** (3 players)
* **30 points** (4 players)

*(Epic Win: A player who collects all 4 Mermaid cards instantly wins the entire game, regardless of points).*
