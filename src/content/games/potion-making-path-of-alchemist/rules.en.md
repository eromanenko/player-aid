*Note: This is an expansion for Potion-Making: Practice. It follows the base game rules with the following additions:*

## Setup
Shuffle the Path of the Alchemist deck separately. Deal 2 cards from this expansion deck and 4 cards from the base game deck to each player. The "Desk of Elements" starts empty (but you will still deal the initial 4 base cards there as usual). 

## New Gameplay Rules
On your turn, you draw **1 card from either deck** (Base or Alchemist), and then play 1 card from your hand. You have a total hand limit of 7 cards (from either deck) at the end of your turn.

**The Alchemist Cards:**
- These cards contain complex new recipes (like Elixir of Omniscience) that require combinations of potions from the base game.
- You can play Alchemist cards as Elements to the Desk, just like base cards.

**The Great Work:**
The ultimate goal of alchemy! Players can create "The Great Work" (Philosopher's Stone) in three stages: Nigredo, Albedo, and Rubedo. 
- You must build them in order. 
- Completing a stage requires specific high-level potions.
- Each stage gives massive points.

## Winning
The game ends when BOTH draw decks are empty and players have played all their cards. The player with the most points wins!
