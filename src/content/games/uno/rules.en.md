## Setup
Deal 7 cards to each player. Place the remaining deck face down to form a draw pile. Turn over the top card to begin the discard pile. 

## Gameplay
On your turn, you must play ONE card onto the discard pile that matches the top card by either **color**, **number**, or **symbol**.
- If you don't have a matching card (or choose not to play one), you MUST draw 1 card from the draw pile. If the drawn card can be played, you may play it immediately. Otherwise, your turn ends.

**Action Cards:**
- **Skip:** The next player loses their turn.
- **Reverse:** Reverses the direction of play.
- **Draw Two (+2):** The next player must draw 2 cards and misses their turn.
- **Wild:** You choose the color that continues play.
- **Wild Draw Four (+4):** You choose the color, AND the next player draws 4 cards and misses their turn. (You can only play this if you don't have a card matching the current *color*).

**Yelling "UNO!"**
When you play your second-to-last card, you MUST yell "UNO!" to indicate you have only one card left. If you forget, and another player catches you before the next player begins their turn, you must draw 2 penalty cards.

## Winning
The first player to get rid of all their cards wins the round!
*(Optional Scoring):* The winner gets points for all cards left in opponents' hands. First to 500 points wins the game.

## UNO Flip!
UNO Flip has double-sided cards: a "Light" side and a "Dark" side. 

You always start playing on the **Light** side. 
When someone plays a **FLIP** card, ALL players must immediately flip their hands around! The draw pile and discard pile are also flipped. You are now playing on the **Dark** side, which has much harsher action cards!
- You keep playing on the current side until another FLIP card is played.
- **Dark Actions:** Include Draw Five (+5), Skip Everyone (you get another turn), and Wild Draw Color (next player draws until they get the color you chose).
