## Setup
Place the main board and set the Quest of the Ring track. Choose who plays the Fellowship and who plays Sauron. Set up the Chapter 1 card pyramid (some face up, some face down) as shown in the rulebook. Place tokens and coins.

## Gameplay
Played over a maximum of 3 Chapters. On your turn, you must choose 1 fully accessible card from the pyramid (no other cards overlapping it) and do ONE of two things:

1. **Play the Card:** Pay its cost (in coins or skills you have) and place it in your play area. Cards can give you Skills, Military strength, Ring movement, or Coins.
2. **Discard for Coins:** Discard the card from the game and take coins from the bank (base 2 coins + bonuses).

When a card is taken, any face-down cards underneath that are now fully uncovered are flipped face up.

## Winning
The game can end instantly in two ways:
1. **Quest of the Ring:** Your marker reaches Mount Doom (Sauron) or Rivendell (Fellowship).
2. **Support of the Races:** You collect all 6 different Race symbols on your green cards.

If neither happens by the end of Chapter 3, the game ends. The player who controls the most Regions in Middle-earth (using Military icons and unit placement) wins the game!
