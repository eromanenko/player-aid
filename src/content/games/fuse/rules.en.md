## Setup
Place the Bomb card deck in the center and reveal a market of Bomb cards. Deal starting Bomb cards to each player based on difficulty. Put all the dice in the bag. **Start a 10-minute timer!**

## Gameplay
FUSE is a real-time cooperative game. You have exactly 10 minutes to defuse all the bombs in the deck.
1. **Draw and Roll:** One player reaches into the bag, draws dice equal to the number of players, and rolls them in the center.
2. **Assign Dice:** Players must decide together who takes which die. *Each player must take exactly ONE die* and place it on one of their active Bomb cards. The die must match the color, number, or math requirement shown on the card.
3. **Misfire!** If a die cannot be placed by *anyone*, it is a misfire. You must re-roll that die, and every player must lose a die from their Bomb cards that matches either the color or number of the re-rolled die (returning it to the bag).
4. **Defused!** When a Bomb card's requirements are completely filled with dice, it is defused! Return those dice to the bag, set the card aside (it may give you a special ability), and draw a new Bomb card from the market.

## Winning
If you successfully defuse all the bombs in the deck and the central market before the 10-minute timer runs out, you win! If the timer expires first, the ship explodes and you lose.
