## Setup
1. Take as many "Famous Gangsters" cards as there are players (the rest are not used), shuffle them, and deal one to each player. The player with Al Capone starts the game. If no one has Al Capone, redeal the cards. In subsequent rounds, the player with the highest score starts.
2. Shuffle the bottle and character cards. Deal 4 cards face down to each player (this is your stash). All players place their cards face down in front of them without looking at them.
3. Place 4 cards face down in the center of the table. These cards are in the safe. They can only be looked at using the Safecracker card.
4. Place the remaining cards in a face-down deck in the center of the table.

![Setup](/games/omerta/setup.jpg)

When everything is set up, Al Capone gives the go-ahead: each player looks at 2 of their own cards (any of their choice), memorizes them, and puts them back face down.

## Gameplay
On your turn, you draw one card: either the top card from the deck (look at it without showing anyone) or the top card from the discard pile. Then you must choose:
*   **Replace:** Replace one of your cards with the one you just drew, placing it face down without looking at your cards. Your replaced card goes to the discard pile face up.
*   **Discard:** Put the card you just drew directly into the discard pile face up (only if you drew from the deck, not from the discard pile).

> [!NOTE]
> **Discarding matching bottles:** When a player discards a bottle card, all players (including the active player) have the opportunity to get rid of an identical bottle card from their stash by revealing and discarding it. However, only **one additional** bottle card can be discarded this way: only the fastest player can get rid of their card. 
> *Penalty:* If a player reveals the wrong card for this, it returns to them, and they take another card from the deck as a penalty without looking at it (placed face down). If other players tried to discard but were too slow, they simply return their cards face down to their stash without penalty.

If the deck is empty, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck and continue playing.

### Discarding a Character Card
When the active player discards a character card, that card's ability is activated.

## Calling "Omertà" and Ending the Round
When you believe your cards total 7 points or less, including points from character cards, you can call "Omertà".
> [!WARNING]
> Important: You can only call "Omertà" at the beginning of your turn, **before** drawing a card. The round ends immediately.

Players reveal their hands and count their points (bottles + character cards).
*   If the player who called "Omertà" has 7 or fewer points AND has fewer points than all other players, they win the round and score 0 points.
*   If not, the player with the lowest score wins the round and scores 0 points. The player who called "Omertà" loses the round, scores the sum of all their cards, and receives an additional **20-point penalty**.
*   All other players score points equal to the sum of their cards (bottles + characters).
*   In the event of a tie between the player who called "Omertà" and another player, the caller receives 0 points, and the other player receives their points as usual.

## End of Game
The game ends after a specific number of rounds: you play as many rounds as there are players at the table. The player with the lowest total score across all rounds wins the game.
