## Setup
Shuffle the deck (containing Bottle cards valued 1-10 and Character cards). Deal 4 cards face down to each player in a 2x2 grid. Place the rest as a draw pile, and flip the top card to start the discard pile. *Before playing*, each player secretly looks at the **bottom 2 cards** of their grid and remembers them.

## Gameplay
The goal is to have the lowest total score (fewest bottles) at the end. 
On your turn:
1. **Draw:** Draw 1 card from the draw pile OR the discard pile.
2. **Exchange or Discard:** 
   - You can swap the drawn card with ANY card in your 4-card grid (placing the new card face down, and discarding your old card face up).
   - Or, you can just discard the drawn card face up.
3. **Character Abilities:** If you discard a Character card (from your hand or your grid), its special ability activates (e.g., peek at a card, swap cards with another player, etc.).

**Fast Discarding:** If *anyone* discards a Bottle card, and you know you have the exact same Bottle card in your grid, you can quickly grab it and throw it on the discard pile out of turn! (If you are wrong, you get a penalty card).

## Winning a Round (Calling Omertà)
When you think your total bottle count is 7 or less, you can call "Omertà" at the start of your turn. Everyone else gets one final turn. Then, reveal all cards.
- If you have the lowest score, you get 0 points!
- If someone else tied or beat you, you get your card total PLUS a 20-point penalty!
After a set number of rounds, the lowest total score wins the game.
