## Setup
For 5 players, all cards are dealt. 
For 4 players, cards except ⚄ are dealt.
For 3 players, cards except ⚄ and ⚃ are dealt. 
Sort your cards by value into sets. **Important:** you cannot turn the cards upside down. 
The player who gets the card with a star starts.
Goal: get rid of all cards. 

## Gameplay
On your turn, perform one of 4 actions:
1. Play a set.
2. Add a card to another player's set.
3. Take another player's set into your hand, turning it upside down.
4. Turn all your cards upside down.

**Same Sets Rule:**
There cannot be two sets with the same number of cards on the table at the same time. Therefore, when someone plays a set with the same number of cards but a higher value, you must take your set back into your hand, turning the cards upside down.

**Discarding:**
When your turn comes around again, you discard the set you have lying on the table.

## End of Round and Game
The round ends when a player has no cards in their hand or in front of them.
* **1st place:** gets 2 points for the round.
* **2nd place:** gets 1 point.
* **Others:** get nothing.

The game ends when someone reaches 4 points.
