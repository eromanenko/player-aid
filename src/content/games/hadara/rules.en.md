## Setup
1. **Game Board:** Connect the 5 game board segments together in any order and place it in the center of the table.
2. **Epoch Cards:** Sort the Epoch cards by the numbers on their backs (I, II, and III). Set aside Epochs II and III. Separate Epoch I cards into decks by color and shuffle each deck individually. Draw 2 cards of each color per player and place them face down on the matching colored spaces on the board. Return the remaining Epoch I cards to the box. *(If playing for the first time, remove cards with a star symbol).*
3. **Colony Tokens:** Place the Colony tokens with the coin side up. Sort them into 5 stacks by value. Shuffle each stack separately, then count out a number of tokens equal to the number of players from each stack and place them near the board. Return the rest to the box.
4. **Coins:** Place the coins and "+10" tokens in the supply. Set the scorepad aside.
5. **Player Setup:** Each player takes 1 player board. Shuffle the starting cards and deal one to each player *(use side A for your first game)*. Each player also receives: 1 player aid, 8 bonus tokens (2 of each color), 2 gold medal tokens, and 4 wooden markers (1 of each color).

## Gameplay
Determine the first player. The first player of the current epoch sets the wheel on the board so that each crest points to one of the decks. They choose which decks the crests on the wheel will point to.
In Epoch I, the first player is the participant with the lowest initiative value on their starting card, in Epoch II — the one with the second lowest value, and in Epoch III — the third lowest. Before starting a 4- or 5-player game, give 1 coin from the supply to each participant who will not be the first player in any epoch.

The game is played over 3 Epochs. Each Epoch has two phases:

1. **Phase A (Wheel Draft):**
   - The wheel determines which color pile you draw from.
   - Draw 2 cards from that pile. Choose 1 to keep, and discard the other face-up onto the corresponding board space.
   - For the card you kept: either **Buy** it (pay its cost minus your current Income track level for that color, then place it below your board and increase the shown track) OR **Sell** it (discard it from the game for coins).
   - Spin the wheel and repeat until the deck is empty.

2. **End of Phase A / Income & Military:**
   - Gain coins equal to your Yellow track.
   - Take a Colony or Pillage it for coins (if your Red track is high enough).
   - Carve a Statue for points/bonuses (if your Blue track is high enough).

3. **Phase B (Board Draft):**
   - Players take turns buying *any* of the face-up discarded cards from the board (or selling them for coins).
   
4. **End of Epoch (Feeding):**
   - Repeat the Income, Military, and Culture steps.
   - **Feed your people:** You must have enough Food (Green track) to feed *every* card you bought. If you don't, you must discard cards until you can feed the rest!

## Winning
After 3 Epochs, score points for your cards, carved statues, colonies, silver/gold medals, and remaining coins. The player with the highest score wins!
