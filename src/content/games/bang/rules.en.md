## Setup
Take role cards depending on the number of players:

| Players | Sheriff | Renegade | Outlaws | Deputies |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **4** | 1 | 1 | 2 | - |
| **5** | 1 | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| **6** | 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| **7** | 1 | 1 | 3 | 2 |

Shuffle and deal 1 Role card to each player (keep secret, except the Sheriff who reveals it). Deal 1 Character card to each player. Your character determines your special ability and starting health (bullets). The Sheriff gets +1 bullet. Draw cards equal to your starting bullets.

## Gameplay
Turns proceed clockwise, starting with the Sheriff. On your turn:
1. **Draw:** Draw 2 cards from the deck.
2. **Play:** Play any number of cards. 
   - *Restrictions:* Only 1 "BANG!" card per turn. Only 1 weapon in play. You cannot have duplicate cards in play.
3. **Discard:** Discard cards from your hand until you reach your current bullet count.

**Combat:** 
To shoot someone, play a "BANG!" card. They must be within your weapon's range (default is 1). The target must play a "Missed!" (Mancato!) to avoid losing a bullet. If you lose your last bullet, you die and reveal your role (unless you immediately play a Beer to recover 1 bullet).

## Winning
* **Sheriff & Deputies:** Kill all Outlaws and the Renegade.
* **Outlaws:** Kill the Sheriff.
* **Renegade:** Be the last person alive (must kill everyone else, and the Sheriff last).
