## Setup
1. **First Player:** The last person to cook something becomes the first player. They shuffle the fortune teller cards and place them face down in a stack.
2. **General Components:**
   - Place the scoring track (market board) in the center of the table.
   - Place the flame on the first space of the round track.
   - Place the 4 seal tiles on their corresponding spaces on the scoring track with the "0" side up.
3. **Ingredient Books:**
   - Place the orange and black books on the table — they are used in every game. The black book is double-sided: one side for 2 players, the other for 3-4 players.
   - The other books are grouped into 4 sets (green, blue, red, yellow, and purple), identified by the number of bookmarks at the bottom. Choose sets with the same number for all colors.
   - Place the green, blue, and red books from your chosen set on the table.
   - Set aside the yellow and purple books — they will enter the game in rounds 2 and 3 respectively.
   - Sort the ingredient chips by color and place them next to their corresponding books.
4. **Player Boards:**
   - Each player takes a pot board, a bag, and a victory point marker of their chosen color.
   - Place the VP marker on the seal tile of the same color.
   - Place your pot board in front of you with the side without test tubes facing up, and place your droplet on the "0" space in the center.
5. **Flasks & Rats:**
   - Place your flask on the large stand next to your pot (bottom right) with the full side facing up.
   - Place your rat on the small stand to the left of the flask.
6. **Starting Bags:**
   - Each player takes and places in their bag: **1 ruby, 4 white "1" chips, 2 white "2" chips, 1 white "3" chip, 1 orange "1" chip, and 1 green "1" chip**.
   - *(This starting set is also shown at the bottom of the pot board).*

## Gameplay
Played over 9 rounds. Each round has a **Potions Phase** and an **Evaluation Phase**.

**1. Potions Phase (Simultaneous):**
Players draw ingredient chips from their bags one by one and place them in their pot, advancing along the spiral track based on the chip's value.
- *Push Your Luck:* You can stop drawing at any time. If the total value of **white cherry bomb chips** in your pot exceeds **7**, your pot EXPLODES! You must stop drawing immediately.

**2. Evaluation Phase:**
- **Bonus Die:** The player who reached the furthest space (and didn't explode) rolls the bonus die.
- **Chip Effects:** Evaluate effects of black, green, and purple chips in your pot.
- **Rubies:** If the space right after your last chip has a ruby icon, take 1 Ruby.
- **Victory Points:** Score points equal to the VP value on the space right after your last chip. *(If you exploded, you must choose: take VPs OR buy new chips, you cannot do both).*
- **Buy New Chips:** Use the shopping value on your current space to buy 1 or 2 chips (of different colors) and add them to your bag.

**End of Round:** Spend 2 Rubies to move your droplet forward 1 space (starting closer to the center) or refill your flask. Pass the starting player token.

## Winning
After 9 rounds, players can trade 5 shopping points (coins) or 2 Rubies for 1 VP. The player with the most Victory Points wins!
