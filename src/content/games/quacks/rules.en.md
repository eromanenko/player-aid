## Setup
Each player gets a pot board, a bag, and starting ingredients (1x 1-value green spider, 1x 1-value orange pumpkin, 4x 1-value white cherry bombs, 2x 2-value white cherry bombs, 1x 3-value white cherry bomb). Place your flask on your board. Place the score track in the center.

## Gameplay
Played over 9 rounds. Each round has a **Potions Phase** and an **Evaluation Phase**.

**1. Potions Phase (Simultaneous):**
Players draw ingredient chips from their bags one by one and place them in their pot, advancing along the spiral track based on the chip's value.
- *Push Your Luck:* You can stop drawing at any time. If the total value of **white cherry bomb chips** in your pot exceeds **7**, your pot EXPLODES! You must stop drawing immediately.

**2. Evaluation Phase:**
- **Bonus Die:** The player who reached the furthest space (and didn't explode) rolls the bonus die.
- **Chip Effects:** Evaluate effects of black, green, and purple chips in your pot.
- **Rubies:** If the space right after your last chip has a ruby icon, take 1 Ruby.
- **Victory Points:** Score points equal to the VP value on the space right after your last chip. *(If you exploded, you must choose: take VPs OR buy new chips, you cannot do both).*
- **Buy New Chips:** Use the shopping value on your current space to buy 1 or 2 chips (of different colors) and add them to your bag.

**End of Round:** Spend 2 Rubies to move your droplet forward 1 space (starting closer to the center) or refill your flask. Pass the starting player token.

## Winning
After 9 rounds, players can trade 5 Rubies or 5 shopping points for 1 VP. The player with the most Victory Points wins!
