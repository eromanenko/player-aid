## Setup
Each player places their Runner on the start line of the race track. Players take a starting set of 9 dice (7 Light Grey, 2 Dark Grey). Set up the central market with the 8 colored dice types and their corresponding ability cards.

## Gameplay
The game is played in simultaneous rounds with two phases:

1. **Roll Phase:**
   - Draw dice from your draw zone until you reach your limit (usually 9).
   - Roll all your drawn dice. You can move any dice that rolled "hits" (symbols) to your Active Zone.
   - You may push your luck and re-roll all remaining blank dice.
   - *Busting:* If you re-roll and get ZERO hits, you **Bust**. You lose all dice in your Active Zone to your discard pile, but you move your marker on the Fan Track (which gives bonuses later).

2. **Run Phase:** (Played in turn order or simultaneously if no conflicts)
   - Use your hits to activate abilities on the matching colored cards.
   - Spend **Foot** symbols to move your Runner on the race track.
   - Spend **Coin** symbols to buy up to 2 *different* colored dice from the market.
   - Move all rolled dice (used and unused) and any newly purchased dice to your discard pile. When your draw zone is empty, your discard pile becomes your new draw zone.

## Winning
The game ends at the end of the round when at least one player crosses the finish line. The player who goes the furthest past the finish line wins!
