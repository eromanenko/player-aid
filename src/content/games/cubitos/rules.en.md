A game of Cubitos lasts several rounds. Each round, all players first resolve the Roll Phase, and then the Run Phase. Once all players have completed the Run Phase, the First Player die is passed to the next player clockwise, and a new round begins. Players continue round after round until the end game conditions are met.

## Setup

1. **Personal Setup**
   Each player receives:
   - 2 Runner figures
   - 1 Player board
   - 1 Phase token
   - 7 Light gray starting dice
   - 2 Dark gray starting dice

2. **Choose a Card Set**
   Choose one of the card sets listed on page 8 of the rules. If this is your first time playing, we recommend the "Race 1 (First Game)" card set.

3. **Set Up the Racetrack**
   Place the **Racetrack** corresponding to the chosen card set in the center of the play area. Each player places 1 of their Runner figures on the **Starting Space**.
   Place the **Fan Track** next to the Racetrack. Each player places their other Runner figure on the **Stands**.
   Place the ability cards from the chosen card set around the Racetrack.

4. **Set Up Dice Trays**
   Place each box next to the ability card of the matching color. Put all dice of the corresponding color into each box's tray.

5. **Set Up Token Trays**
   Place the remaining 4 boxes around the play area. Put roughly equal amounts of Coin tokens in two trays. In the other two trays, put roughly equal amounts of Hand (+1) tokens.

6. **Determine First Player**
   Randomly choose one player and place the **First Player die in the Roll Zone** of their player board. You are now ready to begin!

## Roll Phase

1. **Draw Dice**
   Move dice from your **Draw Zone** to your **Roll Zone** until the number of dice equals your **Dice Limit**.
   Your starting dice limit is 9, which increases by 1 for each +1 die token and for each current red line on the racetrack between your Runner and the leading Runner. If your Draw Zone runs out of dice, move all dice from your Discard Zone to your Draw Zone.

2. **Roll Dice**
   Take all dice from your **Roll Zone** and roll them, then check to see if you have **Busted** (see below).

3. **Move Hits to Active Zone**
   Move all your hits (symbols) to the **Active Zone** on your player board and leave all misses (blank faces) in your Roll Zone. Now you must choose to either **Push Your Luck or Pass**.
   - If you **Push Your Luck**, return to Step 2 and roll all dice remaining in your Roll Zone. Repeat until you pass or bust.
   - If you **Pass**, flip your Phase token to the red "Run Phase" side up and wait for all other players to finish the Roll Phase.

> **Bust**
> If you roll all misses and at any point you had at least 3 dice in your **Active Zone**, you bust:
> - You **must** move all dice from your Active Zone to your Discard Zone.
> - You **may** move any dice from your Roll Zone to your Discard Zone.
> - Advance your Runner figure 1 space forward on the Fan Track and gain any coins and steps.
> - Flip your Phase token to the "Run Phase" side up.

## Run Phase

4. **Use Abilities, Resolve Swords, Calculate Total Coins and Steps**
   Players may use abilities on their active dice, then compare the total number of swords from red dice in their **Active Zones** and activate the red card's effect.
   After that, players may continue using abilities. Finally, each calculates their total amount of coins and steps.

5. **Move**
   For each step you have, move your Runner figure 1 space along the Racetrack. You may also spend 4 coins and/or steps to gain 1 step.
   Gain a reward if you end your movement on a reward space.

6. **Buy**
   Each round you may buy up to **2 dice** (they must be of different colors). After purchasing, place these dice in your **Discard Zone**.

7. **Discard**
   Move all dice from your **Active Zone** to your **Discard Zone** (do not discard dice from your Roll Zone), then flip your Phase token to the green "Roll Phase" side up.

## End of the Game

When any Runner's figure reaches the finish space, determine the winner at the end of the current round. The player who crossed the finish space and traveled the furthest distance past it wins!

In case of a tie, all tied players continue racing, playing an additional round.
