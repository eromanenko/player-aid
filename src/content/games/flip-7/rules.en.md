## Setup
Shuffle the deck and choose a player to deal the cards for this round. (The deck contains number cards 0-12, where the number matches the quantity in the deck, and Action/Modifier cards).

The dealer gives one face-up card to each player, including themselves, in turn order. If an action card is dealt during this phase—immediately stop dealing and resolve the effect of that card. After all action cards are resolved, resume dealing until every player has received one card.

> **IMPORTANT!** At this stage, not all players will have a number card. Some players might have a number card or a modifier card, while others might have no cards or even three or four cards, depending on which action cards were dealt.

## Gameplay
The dealer now goes around and offers each player a choice: **"Hit"** (take another card from the deck) or **"Stand"** (pass/exit the round and save your points).

- **Hit (Take a card):** If you choose to take a card, place number cards in a single row, and score modifier cards above them.
  - **Bust:** If you draw a number card that *already exists* in your row, you bust! You score 0 points for the round and are out until the next round.
  - **Action/Modifier:** If you draw a special card, resolve its action or keep it for scoring.
- **Stand (Pass):** You can always choose to stand as long as you have a card in front of you. If you stand, your row is locked in for scoring this round.

*The Flip 7 Bonus:* If you successfully collect 7 *different* Number cards without busting, the round ends immediately and you get a 15-point bonus!

## Winning
At the end of a round, players who didn't bust add up the values of all their Number cards, apply Modifiers, and add any bonuses. First player to reach 200 points across multiple rounds wins the game!
