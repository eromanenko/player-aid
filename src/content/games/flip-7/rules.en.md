## Setup
Shuffle the deck. The deck contains number cards (0-12, where the number matches how many of that card exist in the deck) and Action/Modifier cards. Deal 1 card face-up to each player to start their "line".

## Gameplay
On your turn, you draw the top card from the deck and add it to your line.
- **Bust:** If you draw a Number card that you *already have* in your line, you bust! You score 0 points for the round and are out until the next round.
- **Action/Modifier:** If you draw a special card (like Freeze, x2, +10), resolve its effect immediately or keep it for scoring.
- **Hit or Stay:** After drawing a safe card, you must decide to either draw another card ("Hit") or stop ("Stay"). If you Stay, you lock in your current line for scoring at the end of the round.

*The Flip 7 Bonus:* If you successfully collect 7 *different* Number cards without busting, the round ends immediately and you get a 15-point bonus!

## Winning
At the end of a round, players who didn't bust add up the values of all their Number cards, apply Modifiers, and add any bonuses. First player to reach 200 points across multiple rounds wins the game!
