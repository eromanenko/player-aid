## Setup
Take 5 (also known as 6 Nimmt!) uses a deck of 104 cards, numbered 1 to 104. Shuffle the deck and deal 10 cards to each player. Place 4 cards face up in the center of the table; these are the starting cards for 4 rows. 

## Gameplay
Your goal is to avoid taking cards! Cards have sunglasses icons on them, which count as negative points.

The game is played over 10 rounds (until hands are empty):
1. **Choose a Card:** Each player secretly chooses 1 card from their hand and places it face down.
2. **Reveal:** All players reveal their cards simultaneously.
3. **Place Cards:** Cards are placed into the 4 rows in ascending numerical order, starting with the lowest revealed card and ending with the highest.

**Placement Rules:**
- **Ascending Order:** A card must be placed in a row where its number is *higher* than the last card in that row.
- **Smallest Difference:** If a card can go into multiple rows, it MUST go into the row where the difference between the cards is the smallest.
- **Taking a Row (The 6th Card):** If your card is the 6th card to be placed in a row, you must take the first 5 cards of that row as penalty points! Your 6th card becomes the new starting card for that row.
- **Too Low:** If your card is *lower* than the last card of all 4 rows, you must choose any one row, take all its cards as penalty points, and leave your card as the new starting card.

## Winning
At the end of the round (10 cards played), count your sunglasses (penalty points). Write them down. Play another round. 
The game ends when someone reaches **66 penalty points**. The player with the LEAST penalty points wins!
