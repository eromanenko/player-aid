## Setup

1. **Game Board:** Place the game board on the table.
2. **Windmill Blades:** Turn the 12 windmill blades face down and shuffle them, then place them face up in their designated spaces on the windmill.
3. **Fruit Tiles:** Turn the 42 fruit tiles face down and shuffle them. Divide them into 10 stacks of 4 tiles each. Place one stack in each region on the board and turn the top tile face up. Place the 2 remaining tiles face down in the box so players can't see them.
4. **Finca Tiles:** Randomly distribute the 10 Finca tiles, placing one in each region.
5. **Wooden Fincas:** Place wooden fincas next to the board (amount depends on player count, see table below).
6. **Fruits:** Sort the 108 fruits by type and place them near the board so they are easy to reach.
7. **Action Tiles:** Each player takes the 4 action tiles of their color.
8. **Farmers:** Each player takes farmers of their color (amount depends on player count, see table below).
9. **Bonus Tiles:** Stack the 4 bonus tiles next to the board. The tile with the golden 4 goes on the bottom, then the 5-point tile, the 6-point tile, and finally the 7-point tile on top.
10. **Donkey Carts:** Stack the donkey carts in the center of the windmill (amount depends on player count, see table below).

| Players | Fincas | Farmers | Donkey Carts |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **4 players** | 6 | 3 | 8 |
| **3 players** | 5 | 4 | 6 |
| **2 players** | 4 | 5 | 4 |

*(Note: Starting with the first player, players take turns placing one farmer at a time on any windmill blade and immediately take 1 corresponding fruit from the supply. This continues until all farmers are placed.)*

## Gameplay
On your turn, you must perform exactly ONE of two actions:

1. **Move a Farmer & Collect Fruit:**
   - Choose one of your farmers on a windmill blade.
   - Count how many farmers (of *any* color) are currently on that *same* blade.
   - Move your chosen farmer exactly that many blades clockwise.
   - Collect fruit: Take the fruit shown on your destination blade. You get 1 fruit for *each* farmer currently on the destination blade (including the one you just moved).
   - *Donkey Carts:* If your farmer crosses the dividing line on the windmill, you also take 1 Donkey Cart token.
   - **Market Crash (Shortage):** If there are not enough fruits of a specific type or donkey carts in the center/supply for a player to take, a market crash occurs. All players must return all their tokens of that specific resource to the supply. The player who triggered the crash also returns theirs on the same terms, and only then takes the required amount from the replenished supply.

2. **Deliver Fruit:**
   - Spend 1 Donkey Cart token.
   - You may fulfill up to 6 fruit tiles anywhere on the board by paying the required fruits from your supply. (You can fulfill multiple tiles in different regions if you have enough fruit).
   - Take the fulfilled tiles and place them face down in front of you.

## Winning
The game ends when a certain number of regions have their fruit tiles completely depleted. Players reveal their collected tiles and score points based on the numbers on the tiles (and sets of 1-6). The player with the most points wins!
