## Setup
Place the starting tile face up in the center of the table. Shuffle the rest of the tiles and place them in face-down stacks. Each player takes 7 meeples of their color (the 8th is placed on the scoring board at 0).

## Gameplay
On your turn, perform these actions in order:
1. **Place a Tile:** Draw 1 tile and place it adjacent to at least one existing tile. The edges must match perfectly (road to road, city to city, field to field).
2. **Place a Meeple (Optional):** You may place 1 meeple from your supply onto the tile you just placed. It can be a Knight (city), Robber (road), Monk (monastery), or Farmer (field). *Rule:* You cannot place a meeple in a feature that already has another meeple in it!
3. **Score Completed Features:** If the tile you placed completes a road, city, or monastery, score it immediately and return the meeples in that feature to their owners' supplies.

**Scoring During the Game:**
*   **Road:** 1 point per tile.
*   **City:** 2 points per tile + 2 points per pennant (shield).
*   **Monastery:** 9 points (the monastery tile itself + 8 surrounding tiles).

## Winning
The game ends when the last tile is placed. Score incomplete features (1 pt per tile/pennant for cities, 1 pt per tile for roads/monasteries). Score Farmers: 3 points for every completed city touching their field. The player with the most points wins!
