## Setup
Build the island of Atlantis in the center of the board using the terrain tiles (sand outside, forest middle, mountain center). Players take turns placing their Explorers (meeples) on the island. *Keep the numbers on the bottom of your Explorers secret!* Then place 2 boats next to the island.

## Gameplay
Your goal is to evacuate your Explorers from the sinking island to the safe coral reefs in the corners of the board. The numbers on the bottom of the Explorers (1-6) represent their point value at the end of the game.

On your turn, perform these 3 steps in order:
1. **Move:** Move your Explorers and/or boats up to 3 spaces total. (You can split this among multiple pieces). Explorers move 1 space per movement point. Swimmers move only 1 space max per turn!
2. **Sink a Tile:** Remove 1 terrain tile from the island (sand first, then forest, then mountain).
   - Look at the bottom of the tile. It might have a green border (play immediately) or a red border (keep in hand to play later).
   - Any Explorers on the removed tile fall into the water and become Swimmers!
3. **Roll the Creature Die:** Roll the die and move the indicated sea creature (Sea Serpent, Shark, or Whale).
   - *Whales* destroy boats (everyone in them falls into the water).
   - *Sharks* eat Swimmers in the water.
   - *Sea Serpents* destroy boats AND eat Swimmers/Explorers!

## Winning
The game ends immediately when the special volcano tile (hidden under one of the Mountains) is flipped over! The island explodes!
Players reveal the saved Explorers on the safe coral reefs and add up their values. The player with the highest total value of saved Explorers wins!
