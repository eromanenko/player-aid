## Setup
You will need a surface of at least 70 x 130 cm for the game.

- A. **Journey Board:** Choose the journey board you will play on. For the first game, we recommend the simplest one - the "Library of Five Worlds". Assemble the board from its three parts. *Along the long sides of the board, there are 16 compartments with Element symbols. These compartments are called the "cupboard".*
- B. **Cupboard of Elements:** Place the 16 starting recipe cards (with a green back and golden corners) face up around the board so that the Elements on the cards cover their corresponding cupboard compartments.
- C. **Talisman Markers:** On the journey board (except for the "Library of Five Worlds" and "Underwater World" boards), find the spaces with a golden dragon symbol and place talisman markers on them. The number of such spaces is indicated next to the board's name.
- D. **Reserve:** Place the power tokens and magemas in the appropriate compartments of the organizer, and place it in a convenient spot for everyone. This is the reserve.
- E. **Decks:** Place the deck boards on the sides of the journey board. Shuffle the green, silver, and gold recipe decks, as well as the spell and talisman decks, and place them face down on their respective boards.
   - *When playing on the "Library of Five Worlds" or "Underwater World" board, you will not need the talisman cards.*
   - *In a 2-player game, return talisman cards with the "3+" symbol to the box.*
- F. **Starting Cards:** Each player takes 2 cards from the green deck, 2 cards from the silver deck, and 1 card from the gold deck into their hand.
   - *If you happen to get 2 identical "green" cards, place one of them at the bottom of the deck and draw 1 card from the top.*
- G. **Starting Magema:** Each player receives 1 magema from the reserve.
- H. **Player Tokens:** Each player chooses a color, takes the tokens of that color, and places one of them on the "Start" space. Determine the first player randomly. They will start their turn...

## Gameplay
Players take turns in clockwise order.
Each player's turn consists of several consecutive phases:
1. **SPELLCASTING (optional):** Play spell cards from your hand.
2. **CREATION (mandatory):** Perform one of two possible actions:
   - Place a card from your hand into the cupboard as an Element.
   - Complete a recipe from your hand.
3. **JOURNEY (mandatory** if you have more than 3 power tokens; **optional** if you have 3 or fewer): Move your tokens on the journey board.
4. **REPLENISHMENT (mandatory):** Draw recipe cards into your hand until you have 5 cards.

## Winning
As soon as a player moves three of their tokens onto different final locations, they immediately win!

## Simplified Variant: "School of Magic"
Sometimes you want to take a break from heavy thinking and journeys, and feel like a cheerful student again. Play by the simple rules of the "School of Magic"!
**Goal of the Game:** The player with the most points on their power tokens at the end of the game wins.

### Game Setup
* Assemble any journey board. Use the cupboard as usual, and place the recipe and spell decks, magemas, and power tokens in the center of the board.
* Return the player tokens, "1" tokens, talisman deck, and talisman markers to the box.
* If you want maximum simplification – remove the Eternal Potions and Eternal Elixirs from the recipe decks before shuffling them.

### Gameplay
Play by the rules of the "Path of the Alchemist" with the following changes:
* Your turn consists of three phases: spellcasting, creation, and replenishment. The journey phase is skipped.
* You can have any number of power tokens. Place them face up in front of you so that all players can see how many you have.
* When you place an Element into the cupboard that isn't already there, you receive 2 magemas instead of magema + 1.
* When you disembody your creature, you receive 1 magema instead of choosing between "magema or spell".
* Any completed Powder can be used as any Potion, regardless of the element symbol.

### End of the Game and Scoring
The game nears its end when any player collects a certain number of power tokens:
* 10 tokens for 2-3 players
* 7 tokens for 4 players

Players finish the current round by taking their turns until it is the first player's turn again.
Then each player calculates the sum of points on their power tokens and adds:
* 1 point for each spell card in their hand;
* 1 point for each pair of magemas.

The player with the highest total score is the winner. In the case of a tie, all tied players share the victory.
