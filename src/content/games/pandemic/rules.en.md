## Setup
Place the board and research station in Atlanta. Shuffle the Infection deck and infect 9 starting cities (3 cities get 3 cubes, 3 get 2, 3 get 1). Assign random roles and deal starting cards to players based on the player count:
- **2 players:** 4 cards each
- **3 players:** 3 cards each
- **4 players:** 2 cards each

You can choose the game's difficulty level by using 4, 5, or 6 Epidemic cards for Introductory, Standard, or Heroic levels, respectively. Return any unused Epidemic cards to the box. Divide the player deck into roughly equal piles, creating as many piles as the number of Epidemic cards you are using. Shuffle 1 Epidemic card into each pile. Stack these piles on top of each other to form the Player deck.

## Gameplay
Pandemic is a cooperative game where you work together to cure 4 deadly diseases. On your turn, do the following 3 steps:

1. **Do 4 Actions:** You may do any combination of the following (the same action can be repeated):
   - *Move:* Drive/Ferry (adjacent), Direct Flight (discard city card to go there), Charter Flight (discard current city card to go anywhere), Shuttle Flight (move between research stations).
   - *Treat Disease:* Remove 1 disease cube from your current city (if a cure is found, remove ALL cubes of that color in the city).
   - *Share Knowledge:* Give or take the City card matching your *current* location from another player in the *same* location.
   - *Build a Research Station:* Discard the card matching your current city to build a station there.
   - *Discover a Cure:* At any research station, discard 5 cards of the same color to cure that disease.

2. **Draw 2 Player Cards:** Add them to your hand (hand limit is 7). If you draw an **Epidemic** card, you must immediately resolve it: increase infection rate, infect a new city with 3 cubes, and shuffle the infection discard pile back on *top* of the infection deck!

3. **Infect Cities:** Draw Infection cards equal to the current Infection Rate and place 1 cube of the matching color on each city.
   - *Outbreaks:* If a city ever needs a 4th cube of the same color, an Outbreak occurs! Move the Outbreak marker, and place 1 cube on *every* connected city. (Chain reactions can happen!)

## Winning & Losing
**You Win:** As soon as all 4 Cures are discovered (you do not need to eradicate the diseases from the board).
**You Lose if:** 
- 8 Outbreaks occur.
- You need to place disease cubes but there are none left of that color.
- A player needs to draw 2 Player cards but the deck is empty.
