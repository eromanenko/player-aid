## Setup
1. Choose or randomly determine who will play as the Fellowship and who as Sauron. Place the corresponding warrior and fortress figures in front of you.
2. Place the game board between you and your opponent, next to the central play area.
3. On the game board, place 2 Fellowship warriors in Arnor and 2 Sauron warriors in Mordor.
4. Assemble the Quest of the Ring track as shown in the diagram and place it below the game board.
5. The Fellowship player receives 3 coins, and the Sauron player receives 2 coins. Form a supply with the remaining coins.
6. Sort the Alliance tokens according to their backs, forming one stack for each race. Then, shuffle each stack separately and place them face down in the dedicated slots above the game board.
7. Shuffle the region tiles and place 3 of them face up on the table. Place the remaining tiles in a face-down stack.
8. Divide the chapter cards into three decks according to their backs (1), (2), and (3), and then shuffle each deck separately.
9. At the beginning of each chapter (1, 2, and then 3), lay out the cards from the corresponding chapter in the central play area, following the provided pattern (a reminder is on the side of the box). Be careful, as some cards are placed face up and others face down. Place the 3 remaining cards face down in the discard pile.

## Gameplay Overview
The game begins with the Sauron player's turn, after which players **take turns** until the end of the game.
On your turn, you must **take a chapter card** or **take a region tile**.

**A. Take a Chapter Card**
From the central play area, choose an **accessible** card — one that is not covered by any other cards. Then play it in your play area **or** discard it.

*   **Play the card in front of you:** Pay the card's cost if necessary and place it in your play area. You may immediately apply the effect of this card.
    *Note: Organize the cards in your play area by color, making sure the card effects are visible.*
*   **Discard the card:** Discard the card face down and take coins from the supply. The amount depends on the current chapter:
    *   Chapter 1 — 1 coin.
    *   Chapter 2 — 2 coins.
    *   Chapter 3 — 3 coins.

Finally, end your turn by revealing any newly accessible face-down cards.

**B. Take a Region Tile**
Choose one of the face-up tiles. Pay its cost and place it in your play area.
Immediately place a fortress figure in the corresponding region of the game board and apply its effects. Finally, end your turn without revealing a new region tile.

## End of the Game
There are 3 conditions for immediate victory:

**1. Quest of the Ring**
*   **For the Fellowship:** If Frodo and Sam reach Mount Doom, they destroy the One Ring, and you win immediately.
*   **For Sauron:** If the Nazgûl catch up to Frodo and Sam, they capture the One Ring, and you win immediately.

**2. Support of the Peoples**
If you collect 6 different race symbols on your green cards, you successfully secure the support of enough peoples of Middle-earth and win immediately.
*Note: The eagle symbol present on one of the alliance tokens is an additional race symbol that counts as 1 of the 6 symbols required to win via alliances.*

**3. Conquest of Middle-earth**
If you are present in all 7 regions (with a fortress and/or at least 1 warrior), you dominate Middle-earth and win immediately.
If none of these victory conditions are met by the end of the third chapter, the player present in the majority of Middle-earth regions (with a fortress and/or at least 1 warrior) wins. In the event of a tie, players share the victory.
