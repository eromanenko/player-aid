## 🎲 Setup
- **2-3 players:** Remove 30 cards with the "4-5 players" symbol (80 cards remain).
- **4-5 players:** Use all 110 cards.
- Shuffle the deck and deal a row of **5 face-up cards**.
- Choose a starting player.

## 🔄 Round Structure
Each round consists of 3 phases:

1. **Bidding:** 
   - The first player bids from 0 to 5 (the number of cards they want to take from the row).
   - The following players must either **raise** the bid or **pass**. Bidding continues for 1 round or until someone makes the maximum bid (5).
   - The winner of the bid takes the declared number of cards from the row.

2. **Building:**
   - *Optional:* Destroy 1 top card of one of your towers (goes to the destroyed pile; "0" cards (tower tops) cannot be destroyed).
   - Place **all** won cards onto your towers:
     - You can have only **1 tower of each of the 5 colors**.
     - Card values in a tower must **decrease** from bottom to top.
     - **Eight (8)** can be placed on a card of any value.
     - **Nine (9)** can be placed on any card except a "0".
     - Card "0" is a tower top; no cards can be placed on it.
   - *Important:* If you cannot place at least one of the won cards, you must **cancel** your entire building phase, return the cards to the row, and lower your bid in the bidding phase.

3. **Cleanup:**
   - Discard all remaining cards from the row into the discard pile.
   - The player to the left of the round's bidding winner becomes the next starting player.
   - *Special case:* If all players pass after a "0" bid from the starting player, discard the row and keep the same starting player.
   - *When the deck runs out for the first time* - shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck.

## 🏁 End of Game and Scoring
The game ends at the end of the round when the deck runs out for the **second time**. The discard pile is not shuffled again. Finish the round with the remaining cards.

**Scoring:**
1. **+1 VP** for each card in towers **without a top** (no "0" card).
2. **+2 VP** for each card in towers **with a top** (has a "0" card).
3. Choose **one main tower**: each card in it gives an extra **+1 VP** (adds up to previous points).
4. **Minus points** for destroyed cards: -1 for the first, -2 for the second, -3 for the third, etc. (e.g., 3 destroyed cards = -6 VP).

The player with the most Victory Points (VP) wins (ties share the victory).
