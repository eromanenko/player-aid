## Setup
Each player takes a player board. Sort the tiles by shape into their corresponding bags.

## Gameplay
1. **Draw:** The active player chooses one of the bags and draws tiles equal to the number of players.
2. **Draft & Place:** Each player selects one tile and places it on their board. 
   - Tiles cannot overlap or hang off the edge.
   - If you cannot place a tile, you skip your turn.
3. **Gardeners:** If two Gardener icons are placed orthogonally adjacent, you earn a 1x1 bonus tile.

*The game ends when a player cannot place a drafted tile. Then, bonus tiles are placed.*

## Scoring
* **Chess Pieces:** 5 points if placed on the central path.
* **Roses:** Points for the size of connected clusters.
* **Mushrooms:** 8 points per column that has at least 2 mushrooms.
* **Trees:** Distance between the two furthest trees in each row.
* **Empty Spaces:** -5 points for each empty square on the board.
