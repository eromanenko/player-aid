## Setup
Each player takes a player board. Sort the tiles by shape into their corresponding bags.

## Gameplay
1. **Draw:** The active player chooses one of the bags and draws tiles equal to the number of players. In the first round, draw one extra tile from the same bag. This way, there will always be one extra tile left in the general supply for the following rounds.
2. **Draft & Place:** Each player selects one tile and places it on their board. 
   - Tiles cannot overlap or hang off the edge.
   - If you cannot place a tile, you skip your turn.
3. **Gardeners:** If two Gardener icons are placed orthogonally adjacent, you earn a 1x1 bonus tile. Bonus tiles are placed at the end of the game, after the last round, before scoring.

*The game ends when a player cannot place a drafted tile. Then, bonus tiles are placed.*

## Scoring
* **Chess Pieces:** 5 points if placed on the central path.
* **Roses:** Points for the size of connected clusters.
* **Mushrooms:** 8 points per column that has at least 2 mushrooms.
* **Trees:** In each row with two or more trees without other trees between them, players count the distance between pairs of adjacent trees (including the cells with the trees) and record the largest value for the row.
* **Empty Spaces:** -5 points for each area of connected empty cells on the board.
* **Bonus Tiles:** -5 points for each unused bonus tile.
