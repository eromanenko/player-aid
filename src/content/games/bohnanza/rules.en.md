## Setup
Deal 5 cards to each player. **Crucial Rule:** You may NEVER rearrange the order of the cards in your hand. You must always play, trade, or discard them in the exact order they were drawn.

## Gameplay
On your turn, follow these phases:
1. **Planting:** You *must* plant the 1st card in your hand into one of your bean fields. You *may* optionally plant the 2nd card as well. (If your fields are full, you must harvest a field first).
2. **Trading:** Reveal the top 2 cards from the deck. You may keep, trade, or donate these two cards, as well as any cards from your hand. Trades can only involve the active player and one other player. 
3. **Plant Traded Beans:** Any beans received through trades must be planted in your fields immediately.
4. **Drawing:** Draw 3 new cards from the deck one by one. You must add these to the **back** of your hand.

**Harvesting:** 
You can harvest a field at any time. Count the number of beans in that field and refer to the card's "beanometer" to see how many gold coins you earn. Flip the scored beans over to their coin side and place them in your personal pile.

## Winning
The game ends when the draw pile is emptied for the 3rd time. The player with the most gold coins wins.
