## Setup
Each player takes:
- a card holder
- a note sheet
- a pencil or pen (not supplied in the game box)

Shuffle all 35 Combination cards into a deck, Cipher side up.
Each player receives a Combination card and places it on the holder in front of them, so as to see only the Cipher side. Players must not look at the Statues side of their own card, as it is the combination they must guess.
Choose a first player who takes the First player tile.
Put the half-medallion tokens on the table, within reach of all players.

![setup](/games/temple-code/setup.jpg)

## Gameplay
Temple Code is a competitive deduction game where you race to guess your secret combination.

On your turn:
1. **Pick Cards:** Choose another player and look at the 2 face-up cards in front of them. 
2. **Take and Give:** Take one of those cards for yourself and place it next to your card holder. Return the other card to that player.
3. **Deduce (Juxtaposition):** Look at the card you just took. Its design gives you clues about your secret Combination card based on the symbols:
   - **Blue Circle:** Indicates a statue that is on BOTH cards in the **exact same position**.
   - **Orange Triangle:** Indicates a statue that is on BOTH cards but in a **different position**.
4. **Mark Notes:** Use your note sheet to cross out impossible statues and narrow down your secret sequence of 3 statues.

## Winning
At any point during the game, a player may attempt to guess the combination on their secret card.
- Whether the guess is correct or incorrect, you get a new Combination card to solve.
- The first player to successfully guess **two** combinations wins the game!

## Solo Mode
**Goal of the game:** Find as many combinations as possible before the end of the game.

**Setup:**
Shuffle all 35 cards. Place the first combination on your holder.

**Game flow:**
Draw the next card to obtain information from it.
Then, on each game turn, you reveal two Combination cards from the deck. You must discard one and will get information from the other.
At any point, you may attempt to guess a combination. If you find it, you score a point.
Whether you have succeeded or not, you then replace your card with a fresh one, then draw the next card to obtain information from it, much like at the beginning of the game.

**End of the game:**
The game ends when the 35-cards deck runs out.

**Your score:**
- **1 or 2:** Wannabe Archaeologist
- **3:** Seasoned Archaeologist
- **4:** Expert Archaeologist
- **5:** Master Archaeologist
- **6:** Jedi Archaeologist
- **7:** Oh... wow.
- **8:** Is it even possible?!
