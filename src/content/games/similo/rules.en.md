## Setup
One player is the Clue Giver; the rest are Guessers. The Clue Giver draws 1 Secret Character card and looks at it. They then draw 11 more cards, shuffle them all together, and lay them face up in a 4x3 grid. The Clue Giver draws a hand of 5 cards.

## Gameplay
Similo is a cooperative deduction game played over 5 rounds. 

Each round, the Clue Giver must play 1 card from their hand as a clue:
- **Played Vertically:** The played character has something **IN COMMON** with the Secret Character (e.g., gender, profession, physical trait, lore).
- **Played Horizontally:** The played character has **DIFFERENCES** from the Secret Character (e.g., they share nothing in common).

After playing a clue, the Clue Giver draws back up to 5 cards.

The Guessers must then discuss and eliminate cards from the grid:
- **Round 1:** Eliminate 1 card.
- **Round 2:** Eliminate 2 cards.
- **Round 3:** Eliminate 3 cards.
- **Round 4:** Eliminate 4 cards.
- **Round 5:** Eliminate 1 card (choosing between the final 2 characters).

*The Clue Giver cannot speak or hint in any other way!*

## Winning
If the Guessers ever eliminate the Secret Character by mistake, **everyone loses immediately**.
If the Secret Character is the last card remaining after Round 5, **everyone wins!**
