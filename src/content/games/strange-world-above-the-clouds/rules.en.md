## Setup
Shuffle the deck. Deal cards based on player count to form the drafting display.

## Gameplay
Strange World Above the Clouds is a drafting and spatial puzzle game. Your goal is to build a 4x4 grid (16 cards total) of creatures and habitats that score the most "Stars".

On your turn:
1. **Draft Cards:** Players draft 3 cards per turn.
2. **Place Cards:** You must place your drafted cards into your personal play area.
   - Cards must be placed orthogonally adjacent to existing cards in your grid.
   - You cannot exceed a 4x4 grid.
3. **Placement Restrictions:** Many cards have specific placement requirements (e.g., must be next to a specific color, or not next to another symbol). 
   - If you cannot (or choose not to) meet the requirements, the card MUST be placed **face-down** (the "Tzimimes" side up). Face-down cards score 0 points at the end of the game!

## Winning
The game ends when all players have completed their 4x4 grid (after 16 cards are placed). 
Count the Stars on your face-up cards. Some cards give flat Stars, others give conditional Stars based on adjacent cards. The player with the most Stars wins!
