## Setup
Place the Start card and 3 Goal cards (face down, one hides the gold) on the table with exactly 7 card spaces between them.
Shuffle the 40 tunnel cards together with the action cards.
Build the role deck and deal starting hands based on the player count as shown in the table below (1 role card is set aside face down):

| Players | Saboteurs | Miners | Starting Cards |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 3 | 1 | 3 | 6 |
| 4 | 1 | 4 | 6 |
| 5 | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| 6 | 2 | 5 | 5 |
| 7 | 3 | 5 | 5 |
| 8 | 3 | 6 | 4 |
| 9 | 3 | 7 | 4 |
| 10 | 4 | 7 | 4 |

## Gameplay
On your turn, you MUST play 1 card, and then draw 1 card from the deck.
A card can be played in three ways:
1. **Tunnel Card:** Add to the maze. It must logically connect to the Start card. Miners want to reach the gold; Saboteurs want to block the path!
2. **Action Card:**
   - *Break Tool:* Place in front of a player. They cannot build tunnels until the tool is fixed!
   - *Fix Tool:* Remove a broken tool card.
   - *Rockfall:* Remove any tunnel card from the maze (except Start/Goal cards).
   - *Map:* Secretly peek at one of the Goal cards.
3. **Pass:** Discard 1 card face down into the discard pile.

## End of Round
The round ends when players reach the Goal with the gold (Miners win!), or when the deck runs out and everyone passes (Saboteurs win!).
- *Miners win:* They draw gold cards and split them (starting with the player who found the gold, moving counter-clockwise).
- *Saboteurs win:* They receive a fixed amount of gold depending on how many Saboteurs there are.

Play 3 rounds. The player with the most gold at the end wins!
