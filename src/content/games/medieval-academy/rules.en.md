## Setup
Place the 7 game boards (tracks) in the center of the table. Each player takes a set of wooden discs in their color and places one at the start of each track. Shuffle the Learning cards and deal 5 to each player.

## Gameplay
The game is played over 6 rounds. Each round has two phases:

1. **Draft Phase:**
   - Choose 1 card from your hand to keep, and pass the rest to the player on your left (or right, depending on the round).
   - Repeat this until you have 5 drafted cards.

2. **Play Phase:**
   - Players take turns playing 1 card from their hand.
   - When you play a card, move your disc up the corresponding track by the number of spaces shown on the card (2 to 5).
   - Some tracks (like Gallantry or Tournaments) give positive points. Others (like the Dragon or Beggar) give negative points if you are in last place!
   - Continue until all players have played 4 of their 5 cards. (The 5th card is kept for the next round).

## Scoring & Winning
At the end of rounds 3 and 6, certain tracks score. The player highest on the track gets the most points (or avoids negative points). Some tracks score *every* round. After 6 rounds, the player with the most Chivalry Points wins the game!
