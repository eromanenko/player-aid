## Goal of the Game
The goal of the game is for all players, working together as a single "brain", to play all 36 cards from their hands and domains. The number of cards in the center of the table must not exceed the established limit.

## Setup
1. Shuffle the 36 playing cards.
2. Deal 5 cards to each player. Place the remaining cards as a face-down draw deck in the center of the table.
3. Agree on the difficulty level you want to play. This will determine the maximum number of cards that can be in the center of the table at the same time:
   - **Beginner:** 7 cards
   - **Experienced:** 6 cards
   - **Expert:** 5 cards
4. Players **cannot** communicate with each other, either verbally or non-verbally.
5. The player who most recently played a board game goes first.

## Gameplay
On your turn, you must perform the following actions:

1. **Play a card:** Place one card from your hand or domain in the center of the table.
2. **Execute action:** Perform the action indicated on the played card.
3. **Check:** Check if you have won or lost the game.
4. **Draw a card (optional):** You may draw one card from the draw deck. This is not mandatory, and you can leave it in the deck for another player.
5. Play passes to the next player in clockwise order.

## Win and Loss Conditions
- **You lose** if, at the end of your turn, the number of cards in the center of the table is equal to or greater than the established limit.
- **You win** if all 36 cards have been played (from hands and domains), the draw deck is empty, and the number of cards in the center of the table does not exceed the limit.
