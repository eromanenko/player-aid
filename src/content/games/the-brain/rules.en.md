## Setup
The Brain is a cooperative card game where players work together to empty their hands. Shuffle the deck and deal a starting hand to each player. Set the target number of allowed "failed" cards for the round based on the player count and desired difficulty.

## Gameplay
Your goal is to play all your cards and empty the draw pile. 

On your turn, you must perform these steps in order:
1. **Play a Card:** Choose one card from your hand and play it face up in the center of the table.
2. **Perform the Action:** Every card has a specific action or rule associated with it. You MUST resolve the effect of the card you just played.
3. **Draw a Card:** Draw one card from the draw pile to replenish your hand.

**Communication:**
Players can communicate and discuss their strategies, but you may not show your hand to others! You must coordinate which cards to play and when to use their effects to help the team.

## Winning (or Losing)
- **Winning:** The team wins immediately if all players successfully empty their hands AND the draw pile is empty!
- **Losing:** If the center pile accumulates the pre-determined number of "failed" or specific penalty cards, the team loses immediately. Discuss what went wrong and try again!
