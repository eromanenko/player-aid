## Setup
Distribute the vampire chits evenly among the players. Each player lines up their vampires face down, revealing only the colors of the two vampires at the extreme ends of their row. Place the 60 grave lids (including 6 rats) over the holes on the board. Each player gets 3 garlic chits.

## Gameplay
On your turn, lift one grave lid to reveal its underside color:
1. **Match:** If the lid's color matches one of the revealed vampires at either end of your row, place that vampire into the grave! Leave the lid off and **take another turn**.
2. **Mismatch (Empty):** If the color doesn't match your available vampires, you may place one of your **Garlic chits** into the empty grave. Then, replace the lid. Your turn ends.
3. **Occupied Grave:** If you accidentally open a grave that already has a vampire inside, you get a wooden stake. (If you collect 3 stakes, every other player gives you one of their vampires!). Your turn ends.
4. **Garlic Found:** 
   - If you open a grave with another player's garlic, that player gives you a vampire!
   - If you open your *own* garlic, every other player gives you a vampire!

## Winning
The game is a memory challenge. You must remember where empty graves of specific colors are, and avoid occupied ones. The first player to successfully hide all their vampires in the graves wins the game!
