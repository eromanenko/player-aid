## Goal of the Game
Players are divided into secret teams: Shogun and Samurais, Ninjas, and the Ronin (plays alone). Your goal is to score the most victory points together with your team, or to be the last surviving player from your team.

## Setup (for 4-7 players)
1. **Roles:** Take the role cards depending on the number of players and deal one to each player face down. The Shogun reveals their role, the rest remain secret.
   - **4 players:** Shogun, 1 Samurai, 2 Ninjas (discard 1 Ninja face down).
   - **5 players:** Shogun, 1 Samurai, Ronin, 2 Ninjas.
   - **6 players:** Shogun, 1 Samurai, Ronin, 3 Ninjas.
   - **7 players:** Shogun, 2 Samurais, Ronin, 3 Ninjas.
2. **Characters:** Deal 1 character card face up to each player. Read the name and ability aloud. Place as many resilience tokens (hearts) on it as indicated.
3. **Honor Tokens:** The Shogun takes **5** honor tokens. Everyone else takes **4** tokens (if playing with 4 or 5 players, they take **3** tokens).
4. **Starting Hand:** Shuffle the deck and deal:
   - Shogun — 4 cards;
   - 2nd & 3rd players — 5 cards;
   - 4th & 5th players — 6 cards;
   - 6th & 7th players — 7 cards.

## Gameplay
On your turn, follow these steps:
1. **Recover:** If you have 0 resilience, you fully recover it.
2. **Draw:** Draw 2 cards from the deck.
3. **Play:** Play any number of cards. **Rule:** You may only play 1 weapon card per turn.
4. **Discard:** Discard excess cards. Your hand limit equals your current resilience.

*If a player loses all resilience (0 hearts), they are defeated. The attacker takes exactly 1 honor token from them. The defeated player skips their next turn but fully recovers at the start of their next turn.*
*If the deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile. Every player must discard 1 honor token!*

## End of Game and Scoring
The game ends immediately when at least one player has no honor tokens left. Everyone reveals their roles and counts points.
**Team Victory Points** = Sum of its members' honor tokens × Multiplier.

| Role | 3 players | 4 players | 5 players | 6 players | 7 players |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Shogun** | x 2 | x 1 | x 1 | x 1 | x 1 |
| **Samurai** | - | x 2 | x 1 | x 2 | x 1 |
| **Ninja** | x 1 | x 1 / x 2* | x 1 | x 1 | x 1 |
| **Ronin** | - | - | x 2 | x 3 | x 3 |

*\* In a 4-player game: The Ninja with less honor gets a x1 multiplier, while the one with more gets x2.*
