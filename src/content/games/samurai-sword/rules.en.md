## Setup
Deal Role cards secretly (Shogun, Samurai, Ninja, Ronin), except the Shogun who reveals their role immediately. Deal a Character card to each player face up (determines max health and special ability). Give each player Honor points (based on player count/role) and Resilience tokens (hearts). Shuffle the playing deck and deal starting hands.

## Gameplay
Samurai Sword uses the *Bang!* system, but there is **no player elimination**.

On your turn:
1. **Recover:** If you have 0 Resilience (hearts), you recover them all.
2. **Draw:** Draw 2 cards from the deck.
3. **Play:** Play any number of cards from your hand. 
   - *Weapon Cards:* You can play exactly ONE weapon card per turn to attack another player. The target must be within your weapon's reach (distance). Distance is counted around the table.
   - *Action/Property Cards:* Play for special effects (healing, stealing, extra attacks).
4. **Discard:** Discard down to your hand limit (which equals your current Resilience).

**Defeating a Player:**
If an attack drops a player to 0 Resilience, they are "Defeated". The attacker takes exactly **1 Honor point** from the defeated player. The defeated player skips their next turn's actions, but will recover at the start of their *following* turn.

## Winning
The game ends immediately when any player runs out of Honor points. 
At that moment, teams reveal their roles and sum up their Honor points (applying multipliers for certain roles depending on player count). The team with the most Honor points wins!
