## Setup
Players split into two roles (roles rotate each round): **Painters** and **Museum Directors**. 
The Museum Directors draw 2 Theme cards and place them face up. They then decide how many total paintings (cards) they want from the Painters (typically 2 to 7).

## Gameplay
1. **Submit Art:** The Painters secretly select cards from their hands that best match the 2 Theme cards. They place these cards face down to reach the number requested by the Directors. (Painters cannot show their cards, but can vaguely discuss confidence).
2. **The Forgery:** Draw 4 random cards from the deck (Belratti's forgeries) and add them to the Painters' submitted cards. Shuffle this pool of cards thoroughly.
3. **The Exhibition:** The Museum Directors reveal the shuffled cards and discuss. They must guess which cards were submitted by the Painters and assign them to the 2 Theme cards. The 4 forgeries should ideally be left unassigned.

**Scoring:**
*   **Success:** Each correct guess (a Painter's card correctly identified) earns **1 point for the team**.
*   **Failure:** Each incorrect guess (a Belratti forgery assigned to a theme) earns **1 point for Belratti**.

## Winning
The game ends if Belratti reaches 15 points (you lose!) or when the deck runs out. If you survive, count your team's total points and check the rulebook's tier list to see how successful your museum was!
