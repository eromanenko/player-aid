## Setup
Four ability cards (jokers) are placed in the center of the table. The Belratti card is placed above them.
Next, shuffle the painting cards and deal them face down to the players. Character cards are distributed randomly. The number of painting cards for each player and the distribution of roles depend on the number of participants:

| Players | Painting Cards Each | Museum Directors (Cat) | Painters (Owl) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **3** | 18 | 2 | 1 |
| **4** | 9 | 2 | 2 |
| **5** | 9 | 3 | 2 |
| **6** | 6 | 3 | 3 |
| **7** | 6 | 4 | 3 |

The remaining painting cards form a draw pile, placed face down.

After each round, all character cards are passed to the next player clockwise (to the left). Throughout the game, each player will be in each role multiple times.

At the beginning of each round, the Museum Directors draw 2 Theme cards and place them face up. They then decide how many total paintings (cards) they want from the Painters (typically 2 to 7).

## Gameplay
1. **Submit Art:** The Painters secretly select cards from their hands that best match the 2 Theme cards. They place these cards face down to reach the number requested by the Directors. (Painters cannot show their cards, but can vaguely discuss confidence).
2. **The Forgery:** Draw 4 random cards from the deck (Belratti's forgeries) and add them to the Painters' submitted cards. Shuffle this pool of cards thoroughly.
3. **The Exhibition:** The Museum Directors reveal the shuffled cards and discuss. They must guess which cards were submitted by the Painters and assign them to the 2 Theme cards. The 4 forgeries should ideally be left unassigned.

**Scoring:**
*   **Success:** Each correct guess (a Painter's card correctly identified) earns **1 point for the team**.
*   Each Painter's card assigned to the wrong theme **earns 0 points** for the team.
*   **Failure:** Each incorrect guess (a Belratti forgery assigned to a theme) earns **1 point for Belratti**.

## End of Game
The game ends if Belratti reaches 6 or more points. Count your team's total points and check the table below to see how successful your museum was!

| Points | Visitors' Comment |
| :---: | :--- |
| **30+** | A sight for the gods! |
| **25–29** | Picture perfect |
| **20–24** | By the book! |
| **15–19** | You got the essence! |
| **10–14** | You won't make a living on this |
| **0–9** | A true picture of despair |
