## Setup
Shuffle the Castle (grey) and Village (brown) decks separately. Reveal 3 cards from each to form two market rows. Place the Herald figure next to one of the rows. Each player starts with 15 gold and 2 keys.

## Gameplay
Your goal is to build a 3x3 grid of cards. On your turn, perform these two steps:
1. **Optional Action (Spend a Key):** Spend 1 key to either move the Herald to the other row, OR discard all 3 cards in the Herald's current row and draw 3 new ones.
2. **Mandatory Action (Buy a Card):** Purchase one card from the row where the Herald is currently located.
   - *Pay:* Pay the gold cost shown on the card.
   - *Alternative:* Take the card face-down instead. It acts as a blank card, but you immediately gain 6 gold and 2 keys.
   - *Placement:* Place the card in your 3x3 grid. (After the first card, all new cards must be placed adjacent to an existing one).
   - *Ability & Herald:* Resolve the card's immediate ability. If the card shows a grey/brown Herald symbol below the cost, you *must* move the Herald to that row.

## Winning
The game ends after 9 rounds when all players have completed their 3x3 grids. Calculate your final score based on the specific scoring conditions printed on your 9 character cards. The player with the most points wins!
