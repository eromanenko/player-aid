## Setup
Place the factory displays in the center: **5** for 2 players, **7** for 3 players, **9** for 4 players.
Place 4 tiles on each factory display.
Place the central scoring board and fill its supply spaces with 10 tiles.
The game lasts exactly **6 rounds**. Each round has a different "Wild Color" (indicated on the round track: 1-Purple, 2-Green, 3-Orange, 4-Yellow, 5-Blue, 6-Red).

## Phase 1: Acquire Tiles
Players take turns drafting tiles. On your turn, you must choose a color (that is **not** the wild color of the round) and take tiles from one factory or the center:
1. Take **all** tiles of the chosen color.
2. If there are any tiles of the **wild color** on that factory/center, you **must take exactly one** wild tile (if available).
3. The remaining tiles are pushed to the center.

If you are the first to take tiles from the center, you take the starting player token. You immediately lose points equal to the total number of tiles you took this turn.

All acquired tiles are placed directly next to your player board (unlike the first Azul, there are no pattern lines).

## Phase 2: Play Tiles and Score
Players take turns playing **one** tile placement at a time, or passing.
To place a tile on a space numbered "X" (from 1 to 6) in a star of the corresponding color:
* You must spend exactly **X** tiles of that color (including the one being placed).
* You may substitute part of the cost with wild tiles, but **at least 1 tile must be of the actual color**.
* The central star is built using exactly one tile of each of the six colors.

**Surround Bonuses:**
If you completely surround a printed feature on your board (Pillar, Statue, or Window) with tiles, you may immediately take 1, 2, or 3 bonus tiles of your choice from the central supply board.

**Scoring:**
You score **1 point** for the newly placed tile, plus **1 point** for every tile in the same star that is continuously linked to the new tile.

## Phase 3: Prepare Next Round
After all players have passed, players may keep up to **4 tiles** for the next round (place them in the corners of your player board). Any excess tiles must be discarded (penalty: **-1 point** per discarded tile).
Refill all factory displays with 4 new tiles and move the round marker to the next wild color.

## End of Game
The game ends after the 6th round.
Bonus points are awarded for:
* Completely filled stars (14 to 19 points depending on the color, 12 points for the central star).
* Completely covering all "1"s (4 points), "2"s (8 points), "3"s (12 points), or "4"s (16 points) across all stars on your board.

The player with the most points wins!
