## Setup
The first player is the person who last returned from an island unscathed.
1. To create the starting island, shuffle the island tiles and place them land-side up randomly on the board spaces inside the border. But do not look at the other side of these tiles!
2. Place the krakens on the spaces marked with a symbol. Place the whales and sharks next to the game board.
3. Each player chooses a color and takes 10 corresponding adventurer tokens, a player aid, and 2 rafts. Place the remaining rafts next to the game board.
   - *2-player game:* choose 2 colors each — they are considered one color — and take 20 corresponding adventurer tokens, 2 player aids to remind you of your colors, and 4 rafts.
4. Clockwise, each player takes turns choosing one of their adventurers and placing them on a land tile without any other adventurers, until everyone has placed all of them on the starting island.
   - *Once the game begins, you can no longer look at the numbers under the adventurer tokens! Remember them when you place the token.*
   - *5-player game:* once there is one adventurer on each of the 40 tiles, you may place your tokens on tiles that already have one adventurer.
5. Each player takes turns placing 1 raft on empty water spaces next to the island tiles. Continue until everyone has placed both of their rafts.

## Gameplay
Your goal is to evacuate your Explorers from the sinking island to the safe coral reefs in the corners of the board. The numbers on the bottom of the Explorers (1-6) represent their point value at the end of the game.

On your turn, perform these 4 steps in order:
1. **Play a Tile:** (Optional) You may play one green tile from your hand.
2. **Move:** Move your Explorers and/or rafts up to 3 spaces total. (You can split this among multiple pieces). Explorers move 1 space per movement point. Swimmers move only 1 space max per turn!
3. **Sink a Tile:** Remove 1 terrain tile from the island (sand first, then forest, then mountain).
   - Look at the bottom of the tile. It might have a red border (play immediately) or a green border (keep in hand to play later).
   - Any Explorers on the removed tile fall into the water and become Swimmers!
4. **Roll the Creature Die:** Roll the die and move the indicated sea creature (Kraken, Shark, or Whale).
   - *Whales* destroy boats (everyone in them falls into the water).
   - *Sharks* eat Swimmers in the water.
   - *Krakens* destroy boats AND eat Swimmers/Explorers!

## Winning
The game ends immediately when the special volcano tile (hidden under one of the Mountains) is flipped over! The island explodes!
Players reveal the saved Explorers on the safe coral reefs and add up their values. The player with the highest total value of saved Explorers wins!
