## Setup
Select a mission. Deal the required numbered wires, yellow wires, and red danger wires to the players' stands according to the mission rules. Players arrange their wires in numerical order.

## Gameplay
Bomb Busters is a cooperative deduction game. Your goal is to find and "cut" all matching pairs of wires. On your turn:
1. **Point and Guess:** Choose a wire on another player's stand and guess its number. You are trying to find a match for a wire you (or someone else) have information about.
2. **Result:** 
   - *Success:* If you guess correctly, the matching wires are successfully "cut" and removed.
   - *Failure:* If you guess incorrectly, the bomb's detonator track advances.
   - *Explosion:* If anyone accidentally cuts a **Red Wire**, the bomb explodes immediately and the team loses!
3. **Equipment:** As missions get harder, you will unlock equipment cards that give you special abilities or hints to help deduce the wire numbers.

## Winning
You win the mission if the team successfully cuts all the required numbered wires before the detonator reaches the end of its track. If the bomb goes off, you lose and must try the mission again.
