## Setup
Each player takes a screen and a marker. Choose a key color for this game. Sort out all cards that have your chosen key color on the back (or the universal "all keys" icon). Scatter these cards face down in the center of the table.

## Gameplay
The Key is a real-time, simultaneous deduction game. It's not just about speed, but efficiency!

When the game starts, everyone plays at the same time:
1. **Grab a Card:** Take any card from the center that matches your chosen key color or the universal icon.
2. **Examine the Clue:** Look at the clue on the card. It will help you deduce the perpetrator, weapon, time, or escape route (depending on the specific case).
3. **Mark your Screen:** Use your marker to cross out impossibilities on your deduction board behind your screen.
4. **Keep Grabbing:** Keep taking and reading cards as fast as you can.

**Important:** Every card has a numerical "Clue Value" printed on it. The more helpful the clue, the higher its value. 

## Winning
1. **Solve the Case:** Once you think you have correctly deduced all elements of the crime, grab the wooden Key from the center! (Other players can keep playing).
2. **Check the Solution:** Slide your key into the solution board based on your deduced combination. If the colors on the back match, you are right!
3. **Scoring:** Only players who got the correct solution can win. Add up the "Clue Values" of all the cards you took during the game. 
   - The player with the **LOWEST sum of clue values** wins! Efficiency beats speed!
