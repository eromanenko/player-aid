## Setup
Each player receives a Wonder board (random or chosen side) and 3 coins from the bank. Form the Age I, II, and III decks based on the number of players. For the Age III deck, deal random Guild cards equal to the number of players + 2.

## Gameplay
The game consists of 3 Ages. At the start of each Age, players receive a hand of 7 cards. Every turn is played simultaneously:
1. **Choose a card:** Pick 1 card from your hand and place it face down in front of you.
2. **Pass the cards:** Pass the remaining cards to your neighbor according to the arrow on the card draft track. If you chose from the last two cards, discard the final card face down to the center instead of passing it.
3. **Perform an action:** Once everyone has chosen, simultaneously reveal your cards and resolve one of three actions.

**Available Actions:**
* **Build a Structure:** Pay the cost (resources/coins) and place the card face up in your city. Some cards are free. Some cards offer a free construction chain symbol for a future Age.
* **Build a Wonder Stage:** Pay the cost of the **next** available stage on your Wonder board and tuck the chosen card face down under that stage. The card's printed ability is ignored.
* **Discard for Coins:** Discard the chosen card face down to the center and take **3 coins** from the bank.

**Trading:**
If you lack resources, you can buy them from your immediate neighbors (left or right). The default cost is **2 coins** per resource. The neighbor cannot refuse and does not lose the resource (they can still use it).

## End of an Age (Military Conflict)
When 6 cards have been played (the 7th is discarded), the Age ends. 
Players compare the number of their shields (red cards) with their left and right neighbors:
* If you have **more shields**: Take a Victory token (+1 point in Age I, +3 in Age II, +5 in Age III).
* If you have **fewer shields**: Take a Defeat token (-1 point).
* If tied: No tokens are awarded.

## Scoring (End of Game)
The game ends after Age III. The winner is the player with the most total points from:
1. Military Conflicts
2. Treasury (1 point per 3 coins)
3. Wonder Stages
4. Civilian Structures (blue cards)
5. Scientific Structures (green cards: sets of identical symbols and sets of 3 different symbols)
6. Commercial Structures (yellow cards)
7. Guilds (purple cards)
