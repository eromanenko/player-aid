## Overview
**Azul: Duel** is a dedicated two-player version of the original Azul. It introduces a more tactical, competitive drafting experience with new mechanics like Dome Plates and sunlit/moonlit factories.

## Setup
1. Give each player a Player board (A) and the 2 matching Player tokens (B).
2. Place the 4 small Factories and the large Factory (C) in a circle, with the moon sides close to each other.
3. Place the Scoring board (D) next to the Factories, ensuring players face their own color. Each player takes 2 Scoring markers (E) and places them on the 0 and the 5 of their section.
4. Shuffle the 18 Dome-plates and form a facedown pile (F) and put it next to the Scoring board. Draw 3 Dome-plates and place them face up onto the middle spaces (G) of the Scoring board. Unfold the Tower and place it nearby.
5. For your first game, find the 3 Scoring tablets (H) with a specific symbol and place them, symbol-side up, next to the Scoring board (D) as the end game scoring goals. Once you are familiar with the game, choose or randomly determine which sides and which 3 (or 4) Scoring tablets to use.
![Setup](/games/azul-duel/setup.jpg)
6. From the 75 round tiles, set aside the 9 Special tiles as a separate supply (I). Place the Starting player tile onto the matching space (J) of the large Factory. Put the remaining 65 tiles (13 in each of the 5 colors) in the bag.
7. Fill the sun section of the large Factory with 5 tiles* and the 4 small Factories with 4 tiles each (K) by drawing randomly from the bag. *(In the extreme case that all 5 tiles are the same color, return them to the bag and draw until at least one of them is a different color.)*
8. Form a facedown supply with the 20 Bonus chips (L). Place 1 Bonus chip face down on the moon of each of the 4 small Factories (M).
9. Randomly determine a starting player. The other player takes 1 of the 3 visible Dome-plates and places it, in any orientation, onto any Dome space on their player board. Fill the Scoring board by revealing the next Dome-plate from the pile. Then, the starting player does the same by choosing 1 Dome-plate, placing it on their board, and refills the Scoring board.

## Gameplay
The game is played over exactly **5 rounds**. Each round consists of an Acquisition Phase and a Tiling Phase.

### Acquisition Phase
On your turn, you must perform one of the following actions:
1. Place a Player token to take a Dome-plate
2. Pick all tiles of the same color from the sun of a Factory
3. Pick all top tiles of the same color from the moon of all Factories
4. Take a revealed Bonus chip

### Dome Tiling & Scoring Phase
Once all tiles have been drafted:
1. Players place their acquired tiles onto their boards, strictly following the patterns determined by their collected Dome Plates.
2. Points are awarded immediately for completing rows, columns, and specific dome patterns.
3. **Broken Tiles:** Any tiles that you cannot legally place on your dome plates are considered "broken" and result in negative points.

## End of Game
The game concludes after the 5th round. Players calculate final bonus points based on completed patterns and objectives.
The player with the highest total score is declared the master craftsman!
