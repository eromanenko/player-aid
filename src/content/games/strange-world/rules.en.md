## Setup
- Shuffle the starting Land cards (the 4 cards without Tzitzimimeh on the back) and deal one to each player. Place this card in your play area.
- Set aside the remaining cards, filtering them by player count according to the icon in the bottom left corner (2+, 3+, 4).
- Create the Land deck. Shuffle it and place it face down (Tzitzimimeh side up).
- Place the Dark City cards in a stack in descending order: 9 > 7 > 5 > 4 > 4.
- Place the Fumarole cards nearby.
- Place the Travelers in a stack.
- Randomly determine who gets the Sun card.
> For 3 or 4 players: this player chooses a side of the card, determining the draft direction for the next round and the turn order for playing cards.
- Each player places their starting Land card face up in their play area.

## Gameplay
Each round, deal 3 Land cards face down to each player.
Look at your cards without showing them to other players. Then simultaneously pass 2 of them to the neighboring player in the direction indicated by the arrow on the Sun card.
You now have 3 cards in hand again. Secretly look at your cards. Then simultaneously pass 1 card to the neighboring player in the same direction as before. You now have 3 cards in hand.
Players take turns, starting with the owner of the Sun card and continuing in the indicated direction, playing a Land card face up orthogonally (horizontally or vertically) to any card already placed in front of them. You must respect the placement limits: building a 4x4 square, and beware of the devastation caused by the Tzitzimimeh.

## Winning
The game ends when all players complete their 4x4 grid (after placing 16 cards).
Count the Stars on your face-up cards. The player with the most Stars wins!
